The brief is `docs/briefs/m3.md`, the plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-30-m3.md`, and the rulings are in `docs/superpowers/ledgers/2026-09-30-m3a-myrland.md`.

## Where things stand

- **Branch:** `claude/m3-myrland`, one commit per task. No PR is open.
- **Mýrland**, ten screens south-west of Myrkviðr (grid cols 0–3, rows 8–10), reached by following the forest brook south:
  - **The weir:** a drawbridge over the brook's rapids, hauled up. Its latch is six tiles over the water, so only the boomerang strikes it. It stays down for good.
  - **The ford:** a gravel shoal between rapids, which the spring flood covers. The old plank bridge downstream crosses in every season.
  - **The drowned mill** in its millpond, with its door barred (M3b opens it), and Þuríðr's house beside it.
  - **The lake shore:** Kári's hut and jetty over a cove the warm springs keep open, and Bárðr's landing and ferry.
  - **The warm springs**, the peat cuttings, the bog, and the reed lake, whose islet piece of heart is reachable only over winter ice.
- **Water that behaves:** `rapids` and warm `spring` water never freeze, a `shoal` floods in spring, and plain `water` ices over in winter. The flood never rises round Ask: the stretch they stand in stays dry until they leave it.
- **People (37 in all):**
  - **Kári** lends his rod, buys every catch and waits for Gamli.
  - **Bárðr** rows nobody toward the mountains until the pass is open.
  - **Þuríðr** tells how the serpent sank the mill and that the stone lies in its cellar. This is M3a's goal flag, `q_rs2_mill`. She also offers rest and a save.
  - **Ljótr** warns of the bog-lights and the floods; **Auðr** talks about the islet.
  - **New lines** for Önundr (the pointer south), Sölvi, Eyvindr, Heiðr and Ragna.
- **Fishing:** cast, strike the bite, then reel while keeping the tension in its band and letting the fish run when it surges. Catches depend on season and time of day. Gamli, the old pike, bites in autumn at dawn and dusk, and Kári gives a piece of heart for him.
- **Foes:**
  - **Water-worms** rear out of the rapids and spit mud, the game's first enemy projectile (the shield stops it).
  - **Bog-lights** drift over the peat and the bog at night, fade, flare and dart, and light the dark.
  - Rolled foes come from the new `myrland` spawn table.
- **Gates at hand-off:** `pnpm check` green (1431 tests); Chromium e2e 48/48, twice in a row; budget 489.3 KB gz of 730.
  - The golden hash is unchanged (`f865b677`).
  - `SAVE_VERSION` is still 1. The fixture `v1-m3a.json` and every older one load.
  - The route runs 5,313 ticks (1.5 min of perfect play).
- **Playtime estimate:** M3a adds about 30–45 minutes for a first-time player: the weir, ten screens, five people, learning to fish, the spring detour. Gamli and the winter islet wait on the calendar. That brings the game so far to roughly 3–3.5 hours.

```bash
?preset=myl                                         # the forest brook's bank path: go south, throw the boomerang west at the latch
?preset=myl&screen=myl_ford&at=19,9&season=spring   # the shoal under the spring flood
?preset=fisher                                      # Kári's jetty on an autumn evening: press E, then Enter to cast

PW_CHROMIUM_PATH=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium pnpm e2e --project=chromium
```

## Notes for the next session (M3b)

- **Rulings worth knowing** (details in the ledger):
  - The per-season seam test found a real pre-M3 hazard: the frozen brook ran up into pines. The brook's source is now rapids. Any new seam must pass in all four seasons.
  - The spring detour is the old bridge in `myl_river`, not "north through the springs" as the brief said: the drawn river runs east–west through all of row 9.
  - Fishing: a surge only strains a held line. The first version, which also doubled the fish's run, made Gamli unlandable.
  - Rótarhellir's chests are unopened in presets and in the M2 fixtures, so a proof that needs "no boomerang" must also turn the boomerang chest's gift into cheese (see `NO_BOOMERANG` in `solver.test.ts`).
- **E2E in this container:** a full parallel Chromium run plays the game at about a third of real speed under SwiftShader. Four older specs had waits tuned to a faster machine (the brook's fish, two multi-boot specs, a fixed 100 ms key hold, `walkUntilScreen`); they are fixed, see the ledger. The brook test failed on `main` here as well.
