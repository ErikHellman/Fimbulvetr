## Where things stand

- **Branch:** `claude/project-thread-hru6i7`, stacked on the unmerged M5b and M6a work (the user chose one PR for both, later). No PR and no GitHub Pages deploy without the user's word.
- **The grapple chain** (D4's dungeon item, from the chest at Garmr's feet): hooks iron posts and pulls Ask over pits and water, drags light foes and far pickups in, tears shields away.
- **Rafts** ply the Gjöll's rapids between stops; Ask rides one by standing on its deck as it leaves.
- **Fog rooms:** three rooms where only the lantern's circle is clear; Náströnd fills his own hall with fog at the last third.
- **Foes:** hel-hounds (light, lunging in pairs a beat apart), Garmr (the mini-boss: hide no blade bites until the grapple drags it down by its collar ring) and Thane Náströnd (the boss: a tower shield the grapple tears away and he walks off to fetch; two fog-draugr at two thirds; fog at the last third).
- **The dungeon:** 22 rooms on a 6×4 grid: three small keys, the map, the compass, the big key behind lock C, Ís for good from the chest between the rune-stones (no more staves needed), the stave rack for an empty hand, a crack, a heart container and the piece `hp_d4_r14` over the pits.
- **Story:** Náströnd's fall sets `st_thane_nastrond` (`q_thanes` 1, `q_captives` 2). Past his hall Kolbeinn appears once ("Three more, and you will have done my work for me."), and the rune-stone takes Ask back out to the gate.
- **People:** Ulf and Tófa talk through the bars of their cells before the fall. After it they are home by day: Ulf runs a paid herding round on his own pen (20 silver, replayable) and Tófa sells flatbread and cheese over the field fence.
- **Two fixes found by the real-input route:**
  - A solid foe that walks into Ask (Náströnd fetching his shield) pinned Ask in place until death. `moveAll` now ignores blocking boxes Ask already overlaps.
  - A grapple thrown from a moving raft at nothing (or a foe) left Ask standing on the river. Only a pull to a post now takes Ask off a raft.
- **Core changes, all append-only:** `Thing` `post` and `raft`, `chest.learn` and `chest.text`, `pen.when`, `GateArt` `bars`, `ScreenDef.fog`, `EnemyDef.light`, `Sim.grapple()`. The solver joins raft stops, adds post pulls, and lets `use` scripts set flags, teach galdr and give items. `GameState`, `SaveData` and `ContentDb` keep their shape.

  - `SAVE_VERSION` is still 1. `v1-m6b.json` (new: the M6a save played through the M6b route) and every older fixture load.
