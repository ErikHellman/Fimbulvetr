## Where things stand

- **Branch:** `claude/project-thread-hru6i7`, restarted from `main` after PR #13 (M5a) merged; one commit per task. No PR was asked for, and GitHub Pages stays off until the user has playtested the demo.
- **Arm-rings, seiðr vessels and trials:** owned rings are flags (`w_ring_stamina`, `w_ring_thrift`), worn from the pause menu's Items tab. Stamina halves the roll's cooldown, and thrift takes a quarter off shop prices. A `seidr_upgrade` raises the bar by 5. A trial is a story step that runs sand along the top of the screen; it powers the herding and the axe range.
- **The farm, stages 1–2:** Halvar asks from his bed once Ask is home. The roof costs 150 silver, after which he gets up and works the yard by day. The fold and byre cost 250; with her flock gathered, Hildr then brings it down to the pasture.
- **Trading chain, steps 1–3:** Ulf's bell from the burned fold goes to Hildr for a fleece, the fleece to Jórunn for yarn, and the yarn to Kári for Gamli's bone hook. The hook waits for a seal-hunter past the pass (M6+).
- **Ten side quests:**
  1. Hildr's scattered flock (a herding trial, a piece of heart).
  2. Gyða's four lost leaves (a seiðr vessel).
  3. Önundr's five troll stones (the arm-ring of stamina).
  4. Steinn's clasp, carried to Halvar and the answer back (150 silver).
  5. Geirmundr's grave-ring, laid back on its mound at night (a quiver).
  6. Sigrún's three crates, carried home over the screen edges (a piece of heart, and cheese in her shop).
  7. Þórdís's wild honey, smoked out with the lantern in summer or autumn (a mead horn).
  8. Ketill's axe range, five straw men in 45 s (a piece of heart).
  9. Ragna's amber from the cut reeds, the peat and the spring mud (the arm-ring of thrift).
  10. Eyvindr's burbot through the bay ice on a winter night (a piece of heart).
