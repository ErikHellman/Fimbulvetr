## Where things stand

- **Branch:** `claude/m4-haugar`, one commit per task, M4a and M4b together. No PR yet (the user asked only to proceed with M4).
- **Konungshaugr (D3),** 20 rooms on a 5×4 grid under the King's Barrow, entered through `hau_king`'s slab door once the watch is kept:
  - three small keys (over the hidden floor in r04, across the dark ossuary in r06, and past an eye and its bridge in r10) for locks A, B and C;
  - Haugvörðr, the barrow-warden, guards the bow in r09;
  - the grave-gold in r11 wakes three wights, and the big key appears once they are laid again;
  - the Haugbúi King in r19, the third runestone in r20: Farvegr is learned, Kolbeinn is glimpsed, and Ask stands again at the barrow's door;
  - optional: the compass (r05), the piece `hp_d3_r15`, the cache behind crack `d3_k_r18`, and a west wing with a silver chest.
- **Haugar's bow secrets:** the great cairn opens to an eye (the larger quiver inside), the watchtower's eye lowers a bridge to `hp_hau_watch`, Geirmundr sells red mead and arrows by day, Hrafnkell sells arrows, and draugr archers walk Haugar's summer and autumn nights.
- **Systems:**
  - **The bow:** arrows as ammunition (30, then 50 and 70 with quivers), four ways, over water and pits; they strike foes and switches.
  - **Eyes** carved in stone open only to an arrow.
  - **Pits and the hidden floor:** pits are crossed only by what flies; the hidden floor is always there, and shown only in the lantern's or a brazier's light.
  - **Sleepers and grave-gold:** the dead lie still until the gold is lifted.
  - **Mini-bosses** (the boss bar, but the dungeon's boss still waits), one table of enemy shots (spit, arrow, a returning axe), the draugr archer.
  - **The Haugbúi King:** mail that turns every blow; an arrow in his blazing crown while he lowers his head to charge fells him, open to the blade. Archers climb out of the floor at two thirds; he hurls his axe at the last third.
  - **Farvegr:** readied from the pause menu (the galdr now list there), sung under the open sky, it opens a picker of the woken warp stones and pays its seiðr only on the choice.
  - **The solver** learns the bow (arrow lines of up to 12 tiles to eyes and switches) and mini-bosses, and lost an edge-wrap bug in `besideReach`.
- **Story:** `q_runestone_3` now runs through the barrow to `st_stone3_lit`, with Kolbeinn's cameo and a "To be continued" card before the pass (M5).
- **Gates at hand-off:**
  - `pnpm check` green (2114 tests).
  - Chromium e2e: 59/60 in the full run, all five M4b specs passing. The one failure was M3a's weir spec (walking west onto the drawbridge timed out while vitest was running beside it); M4 does not touch that screen, and it passed 3/3 run alone. Worth watching if it recurs.
