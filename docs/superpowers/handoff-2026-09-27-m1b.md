## How to run and try it

```bash
pnpm dev
?preset=night3            # the third evening: sleep in the bed to start the raid
?preset=raid              # just woken to fire
?preset=morning           # the morning after, by Halvar's bed
?preset=myr               # Myrkviðr after the legend (add &time=23:00 for the night)
PW_CHROMIUM_PATH=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium pnpm e2e --project=chromium
```

**New controls:** K and L use the items in slots K and L (none has a use until the boomerang and braziers in M1c). Tab opens the pause menu, M its map; Left/Right change page; K/L/E put the item under the cursor in a slot, E on food eats it; Esc or Tab closes. After a fall, E or Enter rises again.

**Console** (backquote): `give <item> [n]`, `hp <n>`, `god [on|off]`, `weather <kind|off>`, `kill`, plus the old commands.
