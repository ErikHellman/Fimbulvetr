# Handoff after M2c "Deep Myrkviðr" — 2026-09-27

Read this first when you continue Fimbulvetr. M2 ("Uppvík and the turning world") is complete in three parts. The M2a and M2b handoffs (`handoff-2026-09-27-m2a.md`, `handoff-2026-09-27-m2b.md`) still hold for their parts. This one records where M2c left the project, what is open for the user, and the rulings made.

## Where things stand

- **Branch:** `claude/admiring-bardeen-h3rz93`, with one commit per task and ledgers for M2a, M2b and M2c. No PR is open.
- **Four new screens** off the north road:
  - **The fen** (west): Heiðr the völva's hut, black pools and reeds. A reed islet holds a heart piece behind brambles that only Eldr burns.
  - **The birch glade** (east): a ring of white birches round an old stone. By night the huldra waits there.
  - **The troll wood** (east of the roots): moss, boulders and two trolls at night. A ring of stones guards a heart piece; the troll-stone in its gap can be lifted away.
  - **Shortcuts:** the old southern paths into the fen and the glade lie under brambles, so they only open once Eldr is known.
- **New foes:**
  - **The pack leader** (`vargr_alpha`): howls and two vargr come; a blow mid-howl stops it; a heavy lunge.
  - **The Rime King's raven** (`rime_raven`, at night): circles out of reach, shrieks and calls a vargr, dives, then climbs back open to a blow.
  - With forest trolls from M2a, that makes M2's +3 enemies.
- **The völva:** Heiðr gives a horn and brews red and green mead for silver. For three clumps of fen-moss she brews blue. Fen-moss grows in the fen only in autumn, and comes back every year.
- **The huldra:** she is there only at night, her back to the path so her cow's tail shows. She trades the winter cloak for an unnamed promise, to be collected in a later milestone. Refuse, and she asks again.
- **The vargar hunt:**
  1. Take the notice from the Þing-stone.
  2. Dagný tells how the leader fights.
  3. Kill the leader and its pack in the north road's east clearing.
  4. Bersi pays 60 silver and a purse that holds 300.
- **Gates at hand-off:**
  - `pnpm check`: green, 1233 tests.
  - Chromium e2e: 44/44, twice in a row.
  - Budget: 474.9 KB gz of 730.
  - Golden hash unchanged (`f865b677`). `SAVE_VERSION` still 1; fixtures `v1-m2a/b/c.json` and all older ones load.
  - Routes (perfect play): M2b 10,305 ticks (2.9 min), M2c 2,652 ticks (0.7 min). Both also pass with rolling on (`FIMBUL_ROLLED=1`).
- **Playtime estimate:** M2 adds about 60–90 minutes for a first-time player: Uppvík and its people, the Eldr errand, the hunt, the völva (needs autumn), the huldra (needs a night), and the two new heart pieces. That brings the game so far to roughly 2.5–3 hours.

## Notes for the next session

- **Rulings worth knowing** (details in `docs/superpowers/ledgers/2026-09-27-m2c-deep-myrkvidr.md`):
  - **The solver found a real hole.** The new seams let Ask walk round Önundr's fallen pine, through the fen or the glade. Brambles on those paths fix it, and now make them Eldr shortcuts. Any new seam into the north road must keep this proof green.
  - **Solver models:** it follows `use` warps (the night knock) and treats burnable props as walls until Eldr is known.
  - **Callers:** vargr called by the leader or a raven carry `mem.caller`, so each caller counts only its own.
  - **Herbs** reuse `world.vars` (no save change) and are persisted ids.
  - **Bersi's bounty** comes before his first-meeting lines.
  - **E2E presses** go through the shared frame-based `tap`. M1-era specs held keys for 60 ms and lost presses under the heavier suite.
