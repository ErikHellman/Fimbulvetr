## How to run and try it

```bash
pnpm dev
?preset=d1                # just inside Rótarhellir: seax, shield, lantern
?preset=d1boss            # below the lair's great door, with the boomerang in slot K
?preset=myr               # Myrkviðr: walk north-east to the roots and into the cave mouth
PW_CHROMIUM_PATH=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium pnpm e2e --project=chromium
```

**New controls:**
- K and L use the items in slots K and L. The boomerang flies the way you steer (eight ways), or the way you face. The lantern lights a brazier you face.
- Walking into a locked door with a key opens it.
- Leaning on a root block for a moment slides it.
- In a dungeon, the pause menu's map shows the floor.

## Notes for the next session

- **Golden hash** unchanged through M1c.
- **Rulings worth knowing** (details in the ledger):
  - No `push` HeroMode: the hero stays in `move` and shows `push`.
  - `shutter.opens` is optional; a shutter without it is the far side of another room's shutter, armed at spawn.
  - Locks and shutters between rooms sit on the edge tiles, mirrored under one id.
  - Boss bars sit at the bottom edge.
  - The solver treats root blocks and vines as passable and `clear` traps as open.
- **Dungeon state** goes through `dungeonOf` (writes) and `peekDungeon` (reads for views); `tests/tooling/dungeons.test.ts` enforces it.
- **Persisted ids:** add every new chest, lock, saved shutter, piece and heart id to `tests/content/persisted.test.ts`.
- **Route helpers:** `walkFighting(h, x, y, budget, wary)` looks around every 16 ticks, which is needed near root-biters. `fightNear` ignores untouchable, immortal and boss foes.
