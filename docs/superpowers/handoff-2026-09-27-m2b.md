## Where things stand

- **Branch:** `claude/admiring-bardeen-h3rz93`, continuing after M2a. It holds the M2b ledger (`docs/superpowers/ledgers/2026-09-27-m2b-uppvik.md`) and one commit per task. No PR is open.
- **The road north.** Once the first runestone burns, Önundr saws through the pine across the road on `myr_deep`. Beyond it `myr_north` leads to Uppvík's gate.
- **Uppvík:**
  - **Screens:** the gate, the market square, the hall, and the smiths by the bay, with five interiors: the mead hall, the trading house, the smithy, the rune hall and the hof.
  - **The gate** is barred at night. Knocking gets Bersi to open it.
  - **Twelve townsfolk,** each with meeting, day and night lines (rain lines where they matter). By evening most of them crowd into the mead hall. Rain sends the outdoor ones indoors.
  - **Foreshadowing:**
    - Ragna once met a red-braided girl from Askdalr who asked the price of passage south.
    - Steinn stood with Halvar under the mountain.
    - Glúmr talks about the barred longhouse.
    - Hjalti and Þórdís mention the huldra and the völva.
- **Economy and gear:**
  - **Þórdís** gives the first mead horn.
  - **Hrafnkell** sells red mead (20), green mead (25) and a second horn (40) across his counter, by day only.
  - **Ketill** sells the Uppvík sword (80) and a byrnie (100) at his anvil: outside on a dry day, inside on a wet one.
  - **Mead** lives in horns and is drunk from the Items page.
  - **The Gear page** of the pause menu lists weapon, armour, galdr, seiðr, horns and purse.
- **Galdr:**
  - **Sölvi** teaches Eldr for a stave charred in Skeggi's kiln.
  - **Eldr** costs 2 seiðr. The bolt sets grass and leaves alight, and the fire spreads downwind but never in rain. It also melts drifts and ice, lights braziers, burns brambles, and hurts foes (draugr take double).
  - **The HUD** shows a galdr box and a seiðr bar once Eldr is known.
  - **Refilling seiðr:** prayer at a hof now fills it as well as health.
  - **Saving:** mead-hall benches heal and save; after dark, Ask sleeps till morning first.

## Notes for the next session

- **Rulings worth knowing** (details in the M2b ledger):
  - `stepStory` now spends the press that ends a blocking step. Before this, closing a shopkeeper's last line with confirm also bought the first row (Sigrún's lantern since M1a). Golden and the M1 routes were unaffected.
  - The route buys mead rather than the sword: the 30 silver the M1 route leaves does not reach 80. `uppvik.test.ts` and the e2e cover the sword and byrnie.
  - Shops are `use`s with a `when`. A shopkeeper talked to directly just talks.
  - NPC places read the region's sky (`weather`) even indoors, so an NPC goes in because it rains outside.
  - The e2e now waits for `__fimbul.picker().armed` instead of sleeping. The m2a hof test failed under the heavier parallel run while it slept.
  - Hof prayer fills seiðr; mead-hall rest does not.
- **Persisted ids:** none new (no chests or pieces). New flags: `n_*_met` for the twelve, `w_horn_thordis`, `q_eldr_asked` and `st_eldr_learned`. New shops `hrafnkell` and `ketill`, scripts `shop_hrafnkell` and `shop_ketill`, and quests `q_uppvik` and `q_eldr`, all appended.
