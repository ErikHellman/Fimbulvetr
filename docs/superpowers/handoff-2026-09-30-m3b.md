## Where things stand

- **Branch:** `claude/m3-myrland`, one commit per task. PR #11 carries all of M3 (the user asked for one PR, not watched).
- **Bombs:** ammunition (10, and 10 more per bomb bag), set down with the item key, lifted and thrown like pots. The blast hurts foes and Ask, breaks pots, sets off other bombs, strikes switches and wheels, opens cracks, and clears drifts. Bomb pots, drops (only once bombs are owned) and Hrafnkell refill them; the HUD shows the count.
- **Water levels:** a mill wheel sets `w_d2_level` (0–2) when struck by the sword, the boomerang or a blast. Sluice floors flood and race planks float with it; a wheel never changes the ground under Ask's feet.
- **Sökkva Kvern (D2),** 16 rooms under the millpond, through the door at the end of the plank walk:
  - the map (r03) and key 1 (r05, two switches for the boomerang) at level 0;
  - key 2 (r02) over the planks at level 1;
  - lock A opens the powder store, a trap that gives the bombs;
  - key 3 (r08) behind two mud-crabs;
  - lock B opens the great wheel (level 2, boomerang only);
  - lock C, over the tail-race's level-2 planks, opens the big key's room;
  - the big lock opens on Lindormr's pond, then the second runestone.
  - Two combination rooms: the compass (r04) and the piece of heart (r13). A secret cache (r14) and a shortcut (r04) lie behind cracks.
- **Foes:**
  - **Mud-crabs:** only a bomb cracks the shell; the shield turns their pinch.
  - **Lindormr** hides in four mud mounds and spits. Bombing its mound flushes it out. Phases at 16 hp (it moves between mounds, with a decoy) and at 8 (a charge that a wall stops).
- **Mýrland's bomb secrets:**
  - the cracked rock at the springs opens on a cave with the bomb bag;
  - the peat bank's crack hides `hp_myl_peat`;
  - mud-crabs roam the banks once Ask carries bombs.
- **Story:**
  - `q_runestone_2` runs to the lit stone;
  - Kolbeinn reports two stones to his king;
  - Þuríðr points at the open door and notices the quiet pond afterwards;
  - `DUNGEON_NAMES.d2` is Sökkva Kvern.
- **Gates at hand-off:**
  - `pnpm check` green (1691 tests), and Chromium e2e 51/51.
  - Budget 499.4 KB gz of 730.
  - The golden hash is unchanged (`f865b677`).
  - `SAVE_VERSION` is still 1; `v1-m3b.json` and every older fixture load.
  - The D2 route runs 14,267 ticks (4.0 min of perfect play).
- **Playtime estimate:** M3b adds about 60–90 minutes for a first-time player: 16 rooms, learning bombs and water levels, three combination rooms, the boss and the Mýrland secrets. That makes the game so far roughly 4.5–5 hours.

## Notes for the next session (M4)

- **Rulings worth knowing** (details in the ledger):
  - Without `levels` the solver leaves screens with water out. A dungeon with water is proven `within` its rooms with `levels: true`.
  - Trap shutters on a doorway sit on the edge tiles with the lock. One a tile further in shuts Ask into the doorway.
  - Rolled spawns skip foes whose `needs` Ask lacks.
  - The `clear` signal in the solver needs every live foe's `needs`.
  - Balance changes from the route:
    - Lindormr hides 180 ticks (120 was too short for a walk and a fuse);
    - its mounds never regrow under Ask;
    - the mud-crab has no contact damage.
- **The route's walker** (`route_m3b.test.ts`) has crab and Lindormr tactics a later boss can reuse (`slay`, `guard`, `lure`). It clears D2 on 7 of seeds 1–9; in the other two it dies to spit, because it does not dodge.
