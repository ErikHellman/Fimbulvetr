# Handoff after M2a "Turning world" — 2026-09-27

Read this first when you continue Fimbulvetr. It records where M2a left the project, what is still open and the rulings made during it. The M1c handoff (`handoff-2026-09-27-m1c.md`) still holds for everything older.

## Where things stand

- **Branch:** `claude/admiring-bardeen-h3rz93`, from `main` after M1 (f84d028). It holds the M2 brief and plan, the M2a ledger and one commit per task. No PR is open.
- **The world turns.** With rolling on (every real game; the tests pin it off unless they ask):
  - **Weather** is rolled per region and day from the season's table (clear, rain, wind, fog, snow; `storm` stays story-only), and can change once mid-day. It is drawn: rain streaks slanted by the wind, drifting snow, leaves blown across (snow in winter), and fog that closes in to five tiles around Ask (seven with the lantern). Rain puts out braziers in the open.
  - **Wind** blows one of eight ways per region and day; it bends the boomerang's outward flight.
  - **Seasonal cover:** winter snow over all open ground (70%, the sword clears a path, saved), drifts where maps draw `^` (50%, no blade clears them; Eldr will melt them in M2b), ice on water you can walk on, spring mud along water on wet days (75%).
  - **Spawn tables:** Myrkviðr's five inner screens (road, pines, hollow, clearing, deep) roll foes on entry: vargr by day, and at night twice as many with draugr and **forest trolls**, which the sunrise turns to **stone**: lift one, throw it, and it breaks into five silver and a heart.
- **Title screen:** press any key, then Continue, New game, Load (three slots and the backup autosave, with a summary line each), Import, Export and Settings.
- **Settings menu** (title, and the pause menu's Game tab): language, volume, sharp pixels or fill the window, screen shake, lightning flashes, shield toggle, long days, **colour-blind aid**, and **Controls…**, which rebinds every gameplay key (confirm and cancel keep theirs). Changes apply at once and are stored.
- **Saving to a slot:** pray at the rune-stone in **Gyða's hof** (it heals too) and pick slot 1–3. Mead halls (M2b) use the same step.
- **Gates at hand-off:**
  - `pnpm check`: green, 998 tests.
  - Chromium e2e: 38/38 (new `m2a.spec.ts`: the title, settings and a remap surviving a reload, a hof save loaded from the title, every weather, every screen in a winter night and a wet spring day with rolled foes).
  - Budget: 451.0 KB gz of 730.
  - Golden hash unchanged (`f865b677`). `SAVE_VERSION` still 1; new fixture `v1-m2a.json`.

## How to try it

```bash
pnpm dev                          # the title screen
?preset=turning                   # a winter night on the Myrkviðr road, half an hour before sunrise
?preset=myr&weather=fog           # or rain, wind, snow (add &season=winter for snow cover)
?preset=myr&season=spring&weather=rain&screen=myr_brook   # mud along the brook
?preset=day2&screen=ask_int_hof&at=20,9                    # the rune-stone: pray and save
?rolled=0                         # the fixed M1 world, no rolled weather or foes
PW_CHROMIUM_PATH=/opt/pw-browsers/chromium pnpm e2e --project=chromium
```

## Notes for the next session

- **Rulings worth knowing** (details in `docs/superpowers/ledgers/2026-09-27-m2a-turning-world.md`):
  - The harness and the e2e `boot` pin rolling off (`rolled: false`, `rolled=0`); new tests opt in. `FIMBUL_ROLLED=1` runs the old routes with it on: M1a and M1c pass, M1b's scripted walk is blocked by rolled vargr on `myr_pines`, as expected.
  - Weather conditions read the sky lazily (`CondCtx.weather`), and story weather rules are evaluated without it, so a rule can never depend on itself.
  - A wet day is fixed at 00:00, so mud never flickers during a day.
  - Spawns never touch the combat RNG and come after the screen's own things, so thing ids never move.
  - The picker ignores its first 8 frames, and `storyUi` stops reporting `save` once answered (two bugs found in the browser).
- **Persisted ids:** none new in M2a (no chests or pieces). New item ids are appended in `KEEPSAKES` and `QUEST_ITEMS`.
