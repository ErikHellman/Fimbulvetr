- **Sökkva Hof:** 24 rooms on a 6×4 grid with a three-step water level (`w_d5_level`) raised by wheels; sunken arches only a diver passes; three keys in any order; a fan bridge, a sail raft and twin fans to the great key. Hrönn (mini-boss, needs bombs) and Nykr (boss, needs Vindr and the grapple). The way in is a dive door east of the drowned spire in `sae_drowned`; in winter Eldr melts the ice over it.
- **Home:** Oddr's skiff rows from Bárðr's landing to `sae_landing` outside winter; Hallbera sells red mead at her door.
- **The loom:** three Norn-threads (Myrkviðr's web on `myr_road`, sunk in `myl_ferry`, a night chest in `hau_barrows` for one who knows Ljós) taken to Urðr's well under `sae_well`'s whirlpool weave a seiðr vessel. After that, praying at a hof offers to turn the season.
