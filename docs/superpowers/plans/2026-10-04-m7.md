## System designs

1. **Swimming (M7a).**
   - `TerrainDef.swim` marks deep water a swimmer can cross (`water`, `spring` and the new lake terrains; not `rapids`, `blackwater`, `sap` or pits). `buildCollision` sets a new flag bit `DEEP` (64) on it, beside `SOLID | LOW`.
   - With `sealskin` owned, `heroSolidAt` treats a tile that is `DEEP` and still `LOW` as open. Ice and a resting raft take `LOW` off, so they stay footing to walk on.
   - Ask is in the water while Ask's feet are on such a tile. The hero machine gains two states: `swim` (steered at `Tuning.hero.swimSpeed`, 70% of walking; no sword, shield, item, galdr or interact, all of which already wait for `move`) and `dive`.
   - **Diving:** the roll button in `swim` dives for `diveTicks` (120, 2 s). Under water, foes' blows, shots and fixture hazards pass over Ask (`hurtHero` ignores them while diving), and Ask passes under things marked `overhead` (floating logs, nets). Ask surfaces into `swim` (or `move`, on footing). The roll cooldown follows.
   - **Currents:** `TerrainDef.current: Dir4` pushes a swimmer `Tuning.hero.current` px a tick (0.6); `strong: true` pushes `strongCurrent` (2.2, faster than swimming) and does not touch a diver.
   - **Dive spots:** a `chest` or heart `piece` with `sunk: true` lies on a deep tile under a ripple; a dive over it takes it (no interact). Its id is saved as usual.
   - **Art:** `swim` (4 frames by facing: head and shoulders over a ripple ring) and `dive` (2 frames, `s`: rings and bubbles) for every hero kit.
   - **Solver:** once `sealskin` is held, `DEEP` and `LOW` tiles join the floor (currents included: a strong one is crossed by diving).
2. **The seal-skin quest (M7a).** `marbendill` climbs `nif_shore` at night while `q_sealskin` is under way and drags Ask toward the water; three nights' fights (`q_seal_nights`, int 3); Hrafn gives `sealskin`.
3. **Bárðr's ferry (M7a).** A `use` at `myl_ferry`'s boat runs `ferry_out` (10 silver, not in winter, once `st_pass_open`): a fade and a warp to `sae_landing`. `ferry_back` from the far landing. In winter the landing's water is ice.
4. **Sævatn (M7a).** 13 screens, grid columns 0–2, rows 1–7 (see the brief). Terrains: `lake` (deep, freezes in winter), `warm_ring` (deep, never freezes: `spring`'s look), `current_n/e/s/w` (deep, never freezes) and `current_strong_*`. The integrity test checks seams in every season and with and without the skin.
5. **The Refuge (M7a).** `sae_holmr` and `ref_int_hall`: Embla (`st_embla_found` on first meeting), Vala (free heal once a day, `ev_vala_day`; sells green mead), Hreggviðr (shop priced in `ore`), the war table (a `use` whose script shows `q_captives`/`q_thanes` and the names), a shrine and a warp stone.
   - **Ore:** a counted item `ore`. A shop may set `ShopDef.currency: ItemId` (append-only; default silver).
6. **Vindr (M7b).** `SONGS.vindr`: a gust box the way Ask faces, 5 tiles, for 20 ticks. It pushes foes two tiles (bosses read `mem.gust`), blows `leaves` cover off, spins `fan` fixtures (a fan sets its flag, like a switch) and fills a raft's `sail` (a raft with `sail: true` only sets off when gusted).
7. **Flooded rooms and dungeon currents (M7b).** D5 rooms use the D2 water level (`ScreenDef.water`) with deep terrains that rise; current terrains carry swimmers on fixed lanes; dive passages are deep tiles under an `overhead` wall.
8. **Turning the season (M7b).** A new effect kind `{ k: 'season', season }` (append-only) that calls the clock's `setSeason` and restarts the six-day count. The hof `use`s offer it once `st_loom_woven`.

## Core interface changes (CLAUDE.md "plan first")

**`GameState`/`SaveData`: no change, and `SAVE_VERSION` stays 1.** Swimming, diving and currents live on the hero entity and the tiles. Fixtures `v1-m7a.json` and `v1-m7b.json` are added, and every older one must still load.

**The golden hash stays unchanged:** the golden run never owns the seal-skin, so `DEEP` changes nothing for it, and no old screen changes.

- **`ContentDb`:** no new tables. `TerrainDef` gains `swim`, `current` and `strong`. `ShopDef` gains `currency`. `Tuning.hero` gains `swimSpeed`, `diveTicks`, `current` and `strongCurrent`.
- **`Sim`:** no new methods in M7a (the view reads the hero's state for the swim art). M7b may add `gust()` for the view.
- **`Thing`:** `chest.sunk`, `piece.sunk`; M7b adds `fan`, `raft.sail`.
- **`HeroMode`:** gains `swim` and `dive`.
- **`Effect`:** gains `season` (M7b).
- **SFX:** `sfx_splash`, `sfx_dive` (M7a); `sfx_gust` (M7b). `sfx_seal` exists.
