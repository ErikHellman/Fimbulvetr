# M3 — Mýrland and Sökkva Kvern — implementation plan

## Context

M2 is merged to `main`. Only the user playtests and Swedish proofreads remain open for M1a–M2c. The M2c handoff says: "Next: M3 (Mýrland and D2)… write a brief and get it approved first."

The spec row (§4) asks for:
- **Systems:** water levels, bombs, seasonal fords, fishing, and a ferry gated by `pass_open`.
- **Content:** 10 screens, D2 with 16 rooms, and Lindormr.

The GDD adds:
- Mýrland is wetland and river, in Act I, gated by the boomerang.
- Its fisherman runs a fishing minigame; his catches change with the season, and he gives a heart piece.
- The ferry runs only after the pass opens. Warm springs keep a channel open in an ordinary winter.
- Spring rivers swell and block fords, and winter freezes the rivers.
- Bombs come ×10/20/30 with bag upgrades. They do area damage and break cracked walls, rocks and drifts.
- D2 is Sökkva Kvern: water levels set by mill wheels, bombs as its item, and Lindormr, who hides in mud mounds that must be bombed.

**Approving this plan approves the M3 brief below**, which Task a1 writes to `docs/briefs/m3.md`, as M1 and M2 did.

**Decided with the user this session:**
- **Split, region then dungeon:**
  - **M3a Mýrland:** 10 screens, seasonal fords, fishing, the fisherman, the ferryman who refuses, the boomerang gate.
  - **M3b Sökkva Kvern:** water levels, bombs, 16 rooms, Lindormr, then the bomb secrets back in Mýrland.
  - Each part ends green and playable, with its own ledger, save fixture, handoff and push.
- **Fishing is "bite, then reel":** cast, strike when the float dips, then reel while keeping the line tension in a band. Catches depend on season and time of day, and a rare big fish earns a heart piece.
- **Placement:** south-west, reached by following `myr_brook`'s stream south.

**What the code check turned up (it shapes the design):**
- No ammo, area damage, cracked walls, enemy projectiles or big-lock door exist yet.
- Water is `solid+low`: nothing drowns.
- Switch state is not saved.
- The solver ignores cover, so it never sees winter ice or spring floods.
- `checkEdges` never checks the landing tile. A seam with ice on one side and open water on the other could drop Ask into water, so seams are tested per season.
- There is no `pass_open` flag and no effect that grants a heart piece.
- `myr_brook`'s south row is trees except the stream, so a bank path must be opened.

**Working rules (as in M2):**
- **Branch:** the session's feature branch.
- **Setup:** `pnpm install` first; `node_modules` is missing.
- **Commits:** one per task, with `pnpm check` green before each. TDD for core, content and art.
- **Shell work:** read the matching `node_modules/phaser/skills/<topic>/SKILL.md` first (tilemaps, graphics, particles, input).
- **Pushing:** push the branch at the end of each part, because the container is temporary. Approving this plan counts as the push "ask" in CLAUDE.md. No PR.
- **Ledgers:** `docs/superpowers/ledgers/2026-09-30-m3a-myrland.md` and `…-m3b-sokkva-kvern.md`, one row per task with its rulings. This plan is saved as `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-30-m3.md`.
- **Running out of time:** stop at a task boundary and record the rest in the ledger and handoff.

## System designs

1. **Seasonal water.**
   - **New terrains:**
     - `rapids` and `spring`: `{solid, low}`, in the water auto-tile group. Ice grows only on `water`, so they never freeze and need no new rule.
     - `shoal`: walkable at 0.7, like the ford. The old `ford` is untouched, so M1 and M2 routes don't change.
   - **Cover `flood`:** `{seasons:['spring'], grows:{on:['shoal']}, cut:false, sink:true}`, the mirror of ice's `walk`.
     - `stampCollision` ORs SOLID|LOW onto `sinkTiles`, after `walkTiles`.
     - `refreshCover` holds it off under Ask's feet, as `keepIceUnderfoot` does.
     - It floods every spring day, so it is predictable.
2. **The solver learns seasons.**
   - `solve(db, start, goal, {season, within?})`. It clones the state with that season and reuses the sim's own `buildCover(... derive)`: `walk` tiles become walkable and `sink` tiles blocked, so the solver and the sim cannot drift apart.
   - Story gates (the pine and its brambles, Uppvík's gate, the weir) must hold in all four seasons. A winter bypass is fixed in content (rapids, reeds, brambles) and recorded in the ledger.
   - Winter-only secrets are allowed on an explicit list in the test.
   - The seam test runs per season, and per water level for dungeon grids.
3. **Saved switch and bridge.**
   - `switch.set?: FlagId`: a strike lights it and sets the flag, and it spawns lit if the flag holds.
   - New Thing `{k:'bridge', at, w, h, down: Cond}`, with fixture kind `bridge` (a new `walk` column in `KINDS`). While `down` holds, its tiles lose SOLID|LOW.
   - The solver handles both.
4. **Fishing.**
   - **Core pieces:**
     - Step `{k:'fish', float}`, reached through the `fish_jetty` use (once `n_kari_met`).
     - `StoryRun.fish?: FishRun`, all integers. It is absent when not fishing, so hashes are unchanged.
     - StoryUi `{k:'fish', phase, t, float, tension, band, dist, maxDist, surging, result?}`.
   - **Phases (ticks):**

| Phase | Behaviour |
| --- | --- |
| idle | Confirm casts. |
| cast | 24 ticks. |
| wait | 90–360 ticks, with 0–2 fake nibbles. Striking early spooks the fish. |
| bite | A 20-tick strike window; missing it loses the fish. |
| reel | Tension 0–1000, starting at 400; holding pulls and reels in. |
| outcomes | Tension ≥1000 snaps the line. 45 ticks at 0 tension, or the fish running out too far, means it slips away. Distance 0 means landed. |
| result | Shown for 60 ticks. |

   - **Surges:** every 40–100 ticks a surge adds to the pull and to how far the fish runs; the float jerks as the tell.
   - **Randomness and clock:** the RNG is `state.rng` via `nextInt`, drawn only while fishing, so replays stay exact. The clock is frozen while fishing, as in any story mode.
   - **The fish table** (`content/fish.ts`, `FishDef`, by season and part of the day):

| Fish | Seasons | Times | Silver |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Perch | all | — | 3 |
| Pike | spring, autumn | — | 8 |
| Bream | summer | — | 5 |
| Trout | spring, summer | morning, evening | 10 |
| Eel | summer, autumn | night | 12 |
| Burbot | winter | evening, night | 8 |
| Salmon | autumn | — | 15 |
| **Gamli** | autumn | morning, evening | 50 (weight 1, strong; sets `q_fish_gamli`) |

   - **Rewards:** every catch pays its silver and adds 1 to `q_fish_caught`.
   - **The heart piece:** new Effect `{k:'piece', id}`, using a `grantPiece` pulled out of `collectPickups`. Kári gives the piece through it.
   - **Shell:** the UiScene tension bar and prompts (pure `shell/ui/fishText.ts`); PlayScene draws the float and the line, with the hero's `fish` anim; hook `fish()`.
5. **Enemy spit.**
   - `ActorCtx.shoot('spit', pos, dir)` fires a projectile (enemy faction, 2.5 px a tick, 120-tick life), handled by `stepSpit`.
   - It crosses low tiles and dies on walls. It reaches the hero through `hurtHero`, so the shield blocks it, and a sword strike knocks it away.
   - Lindormr reuses it.
6. **Bombs (M3b).**
   - **Ammo:**
     - `ItemDef.ammo {base:10, bag:'bomb_bag', step:10}`, and `itemMax()` replaces `def.max` in `giveItem` and `buyRow`.
     - `take` keeps ammo at 0 in its slot.
     - Cond `{k:'owns'}`.
   - **Placing:** `USES.bombs` sets prop `bomb` on the probe tile (at most 2 live).
     - `PropDef.fuse` 96, blinking for the last 32 ticks.
     - Lifting and throwing reuse the prop code unchanged.
   - **`stepBombs` → `blast`** (a 40×40 box):
     - Enemies take 8 (`force`, HEAVY); the hero takes 4 (`PIERCE_SHIELD`).
     - Props with `blast` break; switches and wheels are struck; cracks open.
     - Cuttable cover and drifts (`CoverDef.blasts`) are cleared and saved.
     - It emits the `blast` event, a shake and `sfx_bomb`.
   - **`EnemyDef.cracks?: Element`:** a hit of that element breaks the guard.
   - **Cracks:** new Thing `{k:'crack', id, at, w, h, art:'wall'|'rock'}`. Opened ids are saved in `world.opened`, both overworld and dungeon, and a crack on a room edge is mirrored under one id.
   - **Drops:** `DropKind 'bombs'` appears only while bombs are owned, and only in new tables, so old draws are unchanged. `bomb_pot` props hold bombs. The HUD shows the count.
   - **Solver:** once owned, bombs are a capability that is never used up. An integrity rule requires a bomb pot in, or next to, every room that needs bombs.
7. **Water levels (M3b).**
   - **Content fields:**
     - `ScreenDef.water?: FlagId` (`w_d2_level`).
     - `TerrainDef.rise {floods?|floats?: 1|2}`: `sluice`/`sluice_hi` floors flood at level ≥ n; `race`/`race_hi` planks float at level ≥ n. Plus boards, mill wall and silt terrains.
   - **The wheel:** Thing `{k:'wheel', at, level}`. A sword, boomerang or blast strike sets the flag.
     - It refuses (`sfx_block`) if the change would alter the tiles under Ask.
     - The integrity test requires every wheel's neighbours, and every entrance and arrival tile, to be the same at every level.
   - **`refreshWater`** runs in play and story, and returns at once unless the level differs from `LoadedScreen.level`. On a change it:
     - restamps collision (a new `levelTiles` pass);
     - drowns walkers left on water (neither `flies` nor `swims`) as kills;
     - removes resting props and pickups;
     - emits `coverChanged` and `sfx_water`.
   - **Drawing:** a pure `waterIndices()` merges into the cover layer through the existing `setCover` path.
   - **Solver:** it searches a (tile × level) graph. The level dimension opens only once a wheel is reached, and a wheel's move is refused when the tile under Ask would change. The stranded check runs on the same graph.
8. **Big lock (M3b).** `lock.big`: it needs `bigKey`, is not used up, and is saved in `doors`. The solver opens it once the big key is held.

## Risks
- **Winter ice exposing old bypasses.** The per-season proofs in a3 are expected to find some. Story gates get content fixes; secrets go on a winter allow-list.
- **Seam landing (`checkEdges`).** Covered by the per-season and per-level seam tests.
- **Solver cost.** The level dimension opens only after a wheel is reached, and D2 proofs use `within`. Proof times are logged.
- **Level soft-locks.** Guarded by the wheel refusal, level-independent entrances and wheel neighbours, and the stranded check on the tile × level graph.
- **Running out of bombs.** Bomb pots next to every need, mound drops in the arena, and the integrity rule.
- **Fishing on the combat RNG.** It draws only while fishing; documented.
- **Old routes.** The `myr_brook` edit may shift the `route_m1b`/`route_m1c` tick counts. They are logged, not asserted; re-run them.
- **Dialogue volume.** 5 new NPCs, plus new lines for 5 others, in en and sv. The Swedish goes to the user for proofreading.
- **Budget.** Expect about +50–70 KB on 474.9 of 730.
- **Scope.** 33 tasks. Each part ships alone.
