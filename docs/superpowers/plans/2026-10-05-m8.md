## System designs

1. **Heat (M8a).**
   - `ScreenDef.hot?: true` marks a room or screen as hot.
   - The hero entity carries `heat` in ticks. Each tick in a hot screen adds 1. Outside a hot screen, heat drains 4 a tick.
   - At `Tuning.hero.heat` (480 ticks, 8 s), or 1200 with `ember_byrnie` worn, Ask burns: `heatBurn` hp (2, half a heart) every 60 ticks, through `hurtHero` with no knockback. The heat stays full until Ask leaves.
   - `Sim.heat()` returns `{ now, max }` or null for the HUD, which draws a bar under the seiðr bar while heat is above 0.
   - Heat is never saved.
   - **Solver:** hot rooms are walkable. A route through more than one hot room in a row is a design smell, which the D6 content test checks; the solver does not.
2. **Belts (M8a, used in M8b).**
   - `TerrainDef.belt?: Dir4` (terrains `belt_n/e/s/w`) pushes anyone walking on it (the hero, walking foes and props) `Tuning.hero.belt` px a tick (1).
   - `ScreenDef.belts?: { flag }`: while the flag is set, every belt on the screen runs the other way.
   - Belts are ground to the collision grid. The push goes through `movement`, so walls stop it.
   - **Solver:** belts are floor. A belt that runs into a wall is still footing.
3. **Lava (M8a terrain, M8b puzzles).**
   - Terrain `lava` is `{ solid, low, lava: true }`: a pit that glows.
   - Ís lays cover `crust` (new, append-only) on lava in the same 3×3 as it ices water. Crust is walkable and is taken off 360 ticks later by a per-screen timer list on the runtime, not saved.
   - A foe that Vindr shoves onto lava, or that ends on crust when it cools, dies (no drop).
   - **Solver:** with Ís known, lava tiles are footing for a crossing of up to 4 tiles. That is the reach of 6 s of crust at walking speed, after the cast.
4. **Escort (M8a).**
   - `rt.escort`: `{ npc, hp, trail }`, on the runtime only, never saved.
   - A script effect `{ k: 'escort', npc, hp }` (append-only) starts it, and `{ k: 'escort', npc: null }` ends it.
   - The follower walks Ask's trail of positions, 24 px behind. It stops when a foe is within 48 px of it, and waits when Ask is more than 160 px ahead.
   - Through a door or edge it arrives with Ask.
   - Foes' blow boxes and shots hurt it as they would Ask. At 0 hp, the trigger `escortLost` runs the script named in the effect (`lost`).
   - **Solver:** an escort is a script, and the solver treats its destination trigger as reachable when its screens are.
5. **The hammer (M8b).**
   - `USES.hammer` puts the hero in a new state `hammer`: 26 ticks, with the blow on tick 12 in a 20×20 box one tile ahead.
   - What the blow touches:
     - A `stake` is driven down for good: a new thing `{ k: 'stake', id, at }`, saved in `world.opened`.
     - A `crack` with `art: 'floor'` breaks. Only the hammer and Skjálfti break floors; blasts do not.
     - `drift` cover breaks, since it already says `blasts`.
     - A foe takes `Tuning.hero.hammerDamage` (4). An `EnemyDef.armour` foe also has `mem.cracked` set, and from then on the sword bites it.
   - A sword on an `armour` foe that is not cracked glances off, as a block does.
   - **Solver:** stakes and floor cracks open with `hammer` held; armoured foes in a room the solver must clear need the hammer or bombs.
6. **Skjálfti (M8b).**
   - `SONGS.skjalfti` (5 seiðr) shakes the screen (a `shake` event for the view).
   - Every foe without `boss` is stunned for 120 ticks.
   - A boss gets `mem.quake = 1` for its behaviour.
   - Every floor crack on the screen breaks.
   - The stave `stave_skjalfti` sings it once.
   - **Solver:** with Skjálfti known (or a stave held), a floor crack opens from anywhere on its screen.
7. **Forging (M8a).** Sindri's dialogue checks ore and silver (`item` and `silver` conditions), takes both, and gives the gear with the existing `armor` and `weapon` effects. No new shop kind is needed.

## Core interface changes (CLAUDE.md "plan first")

**`GameState` and `SaveData` do not change, and `SAVE_VERSION` stays 1.** Heat, crust, belts and the escort live on the runtime and the entities. Fixtures `v1-m8a.json` and `v1-m8b.json` are added, and every older one must still load.

**The golden hash stays unchanged:** no old screen gains a belt, lava, a stake or a hot mark. `hau_tarn` gains an opening on its east edge, which the golden run never walks.

- **`ContentDb`:** no new tables.
  - `TerrainDef` gains `belt` and `lava`.
  - `ScreenDef` gains `hot` and `belts`.
  - `EnemyDef` gains `armour`.
  - `CoverId` gains `crust`.
  - `Tuning.hero` gains `heat`, `heatEmber`, `heatBurn`, `belt` and `hammerDamage`.
- **`Sim`:** gains `heat()` and `escort()`, for the view.
- **`Thing`:** gains `stake`, and `crack.art` gains `'floor'`.
- **`HeroMode`:** gains `hammer`.
- **`Effect`:** gains `escort`.
- **SFX:** `sfx_hammer`, `sfx_quake`, `sfx_sizzle`, `sfx_bellows`.
