# M10 — Útgarðr and the ending — implementation plan

## Context

M9 is built and up as PR #17, stacked on M8's PR #16. On 2026-10-05 the user asked for every remaining milestone to be built in turn, one stacked PR each, without stopping to ask. The M10 brief (`docs/briefs/m10.md`) takes Claude's recommended answer to each of its questions. This plan turns the brief into tasks and follows the M9 plan's rhythm.

### Working rules (as in M9)
- **Branch:** `claude/m10-utgard`, cut from `claude/m9-hrimfjoll`. One commit per task, with `pnpm check` green before each. TDD for core, content and art.
- **Shell work:** read the matching `node_modules/phaser/skills/<topic>/SKILL.md` first.
- **Pushing:** push the branch at the end of each part, because the container is temporary. One PR for all of M10, based on `claude/m9-hrimfjoll`. GitHub Pages stays off.
- **Ledgers:** `docs/superpowers/ledgers/2026-10-05-m10a-utgard.md` and `…-m10b-ending.md`, one row per task with its rulings.
- **Running out of time:** stop at a task boundary and record the rest in the ledger and handoff.

## System designs

1. **The room-template library (M10a).** `src/content/world/utgard/templates.ts` builds a 40×22 room map from parts, as plain functions over a mutable grid of legend chars:
   - `room(exits)` — walls with doorways on the named sides (a 2-tile gap at the centre of each).
   - `pillars(g, at[])`, `rink(g, box)` (glaze), `pitBand(g, box)`, `lava(g, box)`, `cell(g, box, gateSide)`, `niche(g, at, open)` (an eye niche walled on three sides with clear ice on the fourth, as D7's content test wants), `fill(g, box, ch)`.
   - `done(g)` returns the `readonly string[]` a `ScreenDef.map` takes.
   The 40 rooms are written in TypeScript with it, one file per room as before (`d8_rNN.ts`).
2. **Útgarðr (D8).** 8×5 grid, region `hrimfjoll`, dungeon `d8`, entered from `hrf_utgard`'s gate. The west, east and high wings each end at a seal-stone: an `eye` (lit by a beam, M9) or a `switch` latch (struck), each setting `st_d8_seal_w/e/n`. A rime door into the master-key vault opens on all three (`when` on its `gate`). Four small keys, map, compass, the big key (named the master key in its chest text), heart piece `hp_d8_keep`, a silver cache behind a crack.
3. **Jötunvörðr (M10a).** `jotunvordr` machine: idle → stomp (tell: knee raised, 40 ticks) → a shockwave ring (a projectile ring like Belgr's quake, the hurting radius growing for 24 ticks) → rest. Warded (`guard` 1) while frozen. Eldr thaws it (`EnemyDef.struckBy` the fire tag): thawed, it is open for 180 ticks, then freezes again. A mini-boss in the vault's antechamber.
4. **Kolbeinn's duel (M10a).** `kolbeinn_boss` machine: guarded except when staggered. A three-blow staff chain with a draw-back tell; a parry (as Styrr's) staggers him for `staggerTicks`. At half health he looses a `rime_bolt` between chains (the mirror or a parry turns it, `struckBy: REFLECT` staggers him too). At a quarter he summons two draugr once (`spawn`). At 1 hp he does not die: he kneels (`mem.yield`), turns `immortal`, and his machine emits a `story` event that runs the script `d8_kolbeinn_yield`: a dialogue with two choices, spare (`st_kolbeinn_spared`) or kill (`st_kolbeinn_slain`); both set `st_kolbeinn_beaten` and remove him. **Cut line** applies to the summons.
5. **The binding ring (M10b).** Hrímnir's arena in `d8_r04` (the binding hall). On the runtime: `rt.ring = { cx, cy, r, rMax, rMin }` (px), set by Hrímnir's machine via `mem` and read by a new `systems/binding.ts`:
   - The ring shrinks `ringShrink` px a tick from `rMax` toward `rMin`.
   - Outside it, Ask takes the killing frost: `coldBurn` every 60 ticks (the M9 path).
   - When it reaches `rMin`, Hrímnir breathes: heavy damage (`ringHit`) and knock-back to the ring's centre-south, and `r` resets to `rMax`.
   - `Sim.ring()` returns it for the view (a drawn circle in `BeamView`'s overlay).
6. **Hrímnir (M10b).** `hrimnir` machine, three phases by hp:
   1. **Sweep:** a hand (`hrimnir_hand`, a summoned actor) sweeps across a row (tell: shadow 40 ticks); a sword blow on the hand stuns him → `open` (heart-rune lit, `guard` 0) for 150 ticks.
   2. **Breath** (2/3 hp): adds a breath cone, cast as a fan of three `rime_bolt`s; turned by the mirror, they open him (`struckBy: REFLECT`).
   3. **Pillars** (1/3 hp): drops `icicle`-like `rime_pillar`s whose fallen tiles glaze (`mem.glazeTiles`, read by `onGlaze`), on top of the sweep.
   On death: `st_hrimnir_dead`, the ring is cleared, and `d8_ending` runs.
7. **Spring on the mountain (M10b).** `ClockRules.thaw?: { flag: FlagId; regions: readonly RegionId[] }`. `seasonAt` takes the world's flags (optional) and ignores `fixedSeason` for those regions once the flag is set. Hrímfjöll's `regionWeather` gains real spring, summer and autumn skies (never reached before, since its season was fixed). The solver keeps `fixedSeason` (it never needs the thaw).
8. **The ending (M10b).** Scripts: `d8_ending` (the hall, Embla, the binding closing), warp to Askdalr; `end_home` (the villagers come home), `end_farm` (by `q_farm`), `end_kolbeinn` (by flag), `end_shore` (Embla's "Come with me?", a dialogue choice: `st_end_stay` / `st_end_go`), the final credits (`{ k: 'credits', roll: 'end' }`), then `st_game_done` and a warp to the farm on the next spring morning. The title screen shows a rune when the save has `st_game_done` (M11 if the cut line is hit).

## Core interface changes (CLAUDE.md "plan first")

**`GameState` and `SaveData` do not change, and `SAVE_VERSION` stays 1.** The ring, Kolbeinn's yield and Hrímnir's phases live on the runtime and on entities. Fixtures `v1-m10a.json` and `v1-m10b.json` are added, and every older one must still load.

**The golden hash stays unchanged:** no screen the golden run walks changes.

- **`ContentDb`:** no new tables. `ClockRules` gains `thaw`.
- **`Sim`:** gains `ring()`, for the view.
- **`seasonAt`:** gains an optional `flags` argument.
- **Script step `credits`:** gains `roll?: 'demo' | 'end'`; `CREDITS` gains a sibling `FINAL_CREDITS`.
- **SFX:** `sfx_stomp`, `sfx_breath`.
- **Ids appended:** enemies `jotunvordr`, `kolbeinn_boss`, `hrimnir`, `hrimnir_hand`, `rime_pillar`; the M10 screens, flags, scripts, dialogues and pieces.
