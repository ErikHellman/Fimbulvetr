## System designs

1. **Duels (M5a).**
   - `EnemyDef.duel?: { flag: FlagId; lost: ScriptId }`: a foe nobody dies fighting.
     - Its death is a yield: `killEnemy` runs the thing's `onDeath` as usual, with no drop and no poof sound.
     - In `hurtHero`, a blow that leaves Ask at one heart or less while a duel foe is on the screen ends the duel: the foe leaves, Ask's hearts refill, the duel's flag is cleared and its `lost` script runs.
     - `enterScreen` clears the flag of any duel foe left behind (leaving forfeits).
   - `EnemyDef.parryStun?: number` overrides the parry's stun (a boss otherwise gets half).
   - `styrr_duel` (behaviour `huscarl`): `shield`, `boss.mini` (the bar reads "Styrr"), 12 hp, solid.
     - Stalks with the shield up; close in, a 24-tick tell and a cut (guard stays up).
     - At 6 hp and below, every other attack is a 30-tick wind-up and a HEAVY overhead, his guard down (`mem.open`) while it falls; `parryStun` 60.
2. **Bragð (M5a).** `SONGS.bragd`: the cast pose, then a `bragd` projectile the way Ask faces (4 ways): 4 px a tick for up to 14 tiles, the spin's damage, tag `PIERCE`, gone at walls or on the first foe. It lights nothing.
3. **Hlíf (M5a).** `SONGS.hlif`: `hero.mem.ward` = 3 and `wardT` = 1200. `hurtHero` checks the ward before the shield and the parry: any hit with an amount is absorbed (`sfx_ward`, a `hit` blocked), and the ward loses one. It ticks down in the hero system; the shell draws a ring of runes while it holds. `Sim.ward()` for the view and tests.
4. **The pass (M5a).**
   - Script `pass_open` from a trigger at the door (with `st_stone3_lit`, not `st_pass_open`): the seals flare (`sfx`), a shake, the door's flag, a hold, the event `{t:'breath'}` (the shell's white wave), `setSeason winter`, `st_pass_open`, a card, then the credits step.
   - Story step `{k:'credits'}`, StoryUi `{k:'credits', t}`: it lasts `CREDITS_TICKS`, or until confirm/cancel after a short delay; then an autosave. The shell's UiScene scrolls the credit lines (content `CREDITS` in `content/credits.ts`, bilingual).
   - `GateArt` gains `rime`: the rime wall across the gorge, closed until `st_rime_open` (a flag nothing sets in M5).
   - `hau_pass` rows 0–3 become the gorge (the door at row 4 stays the slab gate).
5. **Askdalr aftermath (M5a).** Content only: `when`-keyed decor and doors for the ruins (the scorched roof, the burned fold and byre, shut houses), keyed on `q_farm` so stages 1–2 flip them. Main quest `q_fimbulvetr`; trigger `home_winter` in `ask_farmyard` once the pass is open.
6. **Rings and seiðr upgrades (M5b).**
   - Owned rings are flags `w_ring_*`. The Gear tab lists owned rings and "none"; command `{t:'ring', id}` wears one.
   - `ring_stamina` halves the roll cooldown; `ring_thrift` takes 25% off shop prices (rounded up).
   - `give seidr_upgrade` raises `maxSeidr` by 5 (up to `MAX_SEIDR`) and fills the bar.
7. **Trials (M5b).** `rt.trial?: { ticks, fail: ScriptId, done: Cond, win: ScriptId }`, started by a story step `{k:'trial', …}` and shown on the HUD as sand running out. Not saved: a reload ends it. Used by the herding and axe trials.
8. **Trade items and quests (M5b).** Item group `TRADE_ITEMS` (appended to `ITEMS`); the trading chain, the farm and the ten side quests are content (dialogue, flags, things), reusing lift and throw, critters, fishing, the troll's sunrise, cover and cracks.

## Core interface changes (CLAUDE.md "plan first")

**`GameState`/`SaveData`: no change, and `SAVE_VERSION` stays 1.** New state lives in flags, items, `world.pieces` and `world.opened`. Fixtures `v1-m5a.json` and `v1-m5b.json` are added, and every older one must still load.

**The golden hash stays unchanged:** duels, the ward and the beam need their flags or galdr, and nothing old changes before the pass opens.

- **`Sim`:** gains `ward()`; `storyUi()` gains `credits` (M5a) and the HUD reads `trial()` (M5b).
- **`EnemyDef`:** gains `duel` and `parryStun`.
- **Story:** steps `credits` (M5a) and `trial` (M5b); events `breath`.
- **`GateArt`:** gains `rime`.
- **Commands:** gain `ring` (M5b).
- **SFX:** `sfx_bragd`, `sfx_ward`, `sfx_seal`, `sfx_breath`.
