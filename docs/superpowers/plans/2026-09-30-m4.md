# M4 — Haugar and Konungshaugr — implementation plan

## Context

M3 is merged to `main` (PR #11). Only the user playtests and Swedish proofreads remain open for M1a–M3b. The user asked to "proceed with implementing M4".

The spec row (§4) asks for:
- **Systems:** the bow, Farvegr and warps, dark rooms, a mini-boss, the huscarl's techniques, and runestone scaling.
- **Content:** 10 screens, D3 with 20 rooms, and the Haugbúi King.

The GDD adds:
- Haugar is barrow hills and stone circles, in Act I, gated by bombs. It holds D3 (Konungshaugr), the old huscarl's cottage and the runestone pass.
- The old huscarl teaches three sword techniques for a price: the dash thrust, the parry, and Bragð (his last lesson, after a duel).
- D3 is a draugr crypt with torches, invisible floors, and grave-gold that wakes the dead if lifted. The bow is its key item, and Farvegr comes with it. The boss is the Haugbúi King: shoot the crown gems while it charges.
- The bow is ranged damage and opens eye switches; arrows come ×30/50/70 with quiver upgrades.
- Warp points: one per region, unlocked on first visit, reached with Farvegr from D3 on.
- A mini-boss guards the key item from D3 on. Lowland difficulty scales with the runestones lit.
- Night-only: draugr rise from mounds in Haugar. "The barrow-watch" is one of the night-only quests.

**Approving this plan approves the M4 brief** (`docs/briefs/m4.md`, written with it).

### Decisions (Claude's calls; the user asked to proceed without a question round)
- **Split, region then dungeon,** as M3:
  - **M4a Haugar:** 10 screens, the huscarl, the barrow-watch, warp stones, scaling.
  - **M4b Konungshaugr:** D3, the bow, Farvegr, then the bow secrets in Haugar.
  - Each part ends green and playable, with its own ledger, save fixture, handoff and push.
- **Placement:** east of Myrkviðr, through the birch glade's east edge. The pass is Haugar's north edge, pointing at the highlands of Act II.
- **Gate:** a rockfall that only a bomb opens, as the GDD's "gate to enter: bombs" asks.
- **Techniques:**
  - M4 teaches the dash thrust and the parry.
  - The GDD's "last duel" gives Bragð, a galdr, and is a character quest. It moves to M5's side quests, since Bragð also needs galdr choice and a sword beam.
- **The barrow-watch opens the King's Barrow.** It is the night-only quest the GDD lists, and the reason the region has a night rhythm. Styrr can wait with Ask until 22:00, so nobody has to idle through a day.
- **Runestone scaling** starts at the second stone (see the brief), so every older route and hash is untouched.
- **Invisible floors** are always walkable. Light only shows them: the lantern's circle, or a lit brazier's. D1 is finishable without the lantern, so D3 must be too. Pits are water-like: solid and open above, so arrows and the boomerang cross them, and nobody falls.
- **Farvegr comes with the third stone** (`stone3_light`), not a chest, so it is learned where the GDD says: at D3.
- **Warp stones are woken by interact,** not by walking past, so the player sees it happen.
- **Mini-bosses** use the boss bar (`boss.mini`), never mark the dungeon's boss dead, and stay dead through a content flag.

### What the code check turned up (it shapes the design)
- **Items:**
  - `arrows`, `quiver`, the `bow` and the `farvegr` galdr ids exist since M0.
  - `world.warps: RegionId[]` is in the save (always empty).
  - Ammo exists (bombs), but the item that fires and the ammunition are the same id there. The bow fires `arrows`.
- **Galdr:** the button sings `inv.galdr[0]`, so readying a galdr is only a reorder: no save change.
- **Collision:** there is no pit or hole flag.
  - Water is `solid + low`, and a pit can be the same.
  - A "ghost" floor is plain walkable terrain that the shell draws as a pit outside the light.
- **Enemies:**
  - Things respawn on every entry, so a mini-boss needs a `when` and an `onDeath` flag to stay dead.
  - `killEnemy` marks the dungeon's boss dead for any def with `boss`, so a mini-boss needs its own mark.
- **Story:**
  - `setMinute` is an Effect already (the wait for nightfall).
  - `Cond` flag compares (`gte`) exist (the watch count).
- **The solver** handles `switch.set`, bridges, cracks, big locks, `within` and water levels. It needs:
  - arrow lines for eye switches;
  - sleepers (grave-gold) as wakeable foes;
  - a mini-boss that is not a boss;
  - the bow as a capability that is never used up.

### Working rules (as in M3)
- **Branch:** `claude/m4-haugar`, one commit per task, with `pnpm check` green before each. TDD for core, content and art.
- **Shell work:** read the matching `node_modules/phaser/skills/<topic>/SKILL.md` first (tilemaps, graphics, input).
- **Pushing:** push the branch at the end of each part, because the container is temporary. Approving this plan counts as the push "ask" in CLAUDE.md. No PR unless asked.
- **Ledgers:** `docs/superpowers/ledgers/2026-09-30-m4a-haugar.md` and `…-m4b-konungshaugr.md`, one row per task with its rulings.
- **Running out of time:** stop at a task boundary and record the rest in the ledger and handoff.

## System designs

1. **Warp stones (M4a).**
   - Thing `{k:'warp', region, at, arrive}`, fixture kind `warp` (always solid; anims `awake`/`dormant`).
   - Interact wakes it: the region goes into `world.warps` (once), with `sfx_warp` and a line.
   - It shows awake while its region is in `world.warps`.
   - The solver treats it as a block.
   - Integrity: at most one per region, `arrive` walkable, on the overworld.
2. **Techniques (M4a).**
   - **The dash thrust:**
     - Hero state `thrust`: sword pressed in `roll` after `thrustFrom` ticks, with `t_dash`.
     - 14 ticks, moving at 0.8× roll speed, a long forward box.
     - Double `comboDamage[0]`, tags `PIERCE` (new), `sfx_thrust`.
     - The roll's cooldown applies after it.
   - **The parry:**
     - `shield` counts its own ticks. In `hurtHero`, a blow from an enemy entity (not a shot, not fire) that meets the shield within `parryTicks` (10) of raising it, with `t_parry`, is parried, even HEAVY.
     - No damage; the attacker gets `mem.stun` 60 (bosses 30); `sfx_parry`, event `hit` blocked.
   - Both are tuning numbers in `Tuning.hero`.
3. **Shields on foes (M4a).**
   - `EnemyDef.shield?: true`: a blow whose direction opposes the foe's facing (dot < −0.3) clinks off, unless it carries `PIERCE`, or the behaviour has set `mem.open` (it is swinging).
   - Checked in `damageActor` before damage, after `guard`.
4. **Runestone scaling (M4a).**
   - `Tuning.stones {flags, hpPct: [100,100,150,200], blow: [0,0,0,1]}` and `ContentDb.lowlands: RegionId[]`.
   - `spawnActors` (authored and rolled) sets `mem.tier` and scales hp for non-boss, mortal foes on lowland overworld screens.
   - `resolveAttacks` adds `blow[tier]` to blows and touches.
   - With fewer than two stones nothing changes.
5. **The barrow-watch (M4a).** Content only:
   - three `haugbui` things with `when` (night, `q_rs3_watch`, not open) and `onDeath: add q_watch_kills`;
   - a whole-screen trigger, when the count is ≥3 and the door is not yet open, runs `barrow_open`;
   - the door is a `gate` (art `slab`, new) closed until `st_barrow_open`.
   - Styrr's "wait for nightfall" choice sets the clock to 22:00.
6. **Bow and arrows (M4b).**
   - **The items:**
     - `arrows`: ammo `{bag:'quiver', step:20}`, base 30.
     - `ItemDef.fires?: ItemId`: the bow fires arrows; the HUD shows that count.
     - `ItemDef.comes?: {item, n}`: giving the bow fills the arrows.
   - **Firing:** `USES.bow` looses an arrow the way Ask faces (4 ways), using one up. With none it clicks (`sfx_fizzle`). Hero state `shoot` (anim `bow`, 16 ticks).
   - **The arrow:** 5 px a tick, 72 ticks; it crosses water, pits and low tiles and dies on walls.
     - To foes: 4 (`pierce` is not an element; `none`, tag `PIERCE`).
     - It strikes switches (eye switches too), and not wheels.
     - It is caught by `mem.gem` on the King.
   - **Drops:** `DropKind 'arrows'` only while the bow is owned. Prop `arrow_pot`. Shops: Geirmundr and Hrafnkell.
   - **Solver:** once the bow is owned, arrows are never used up. An integrity rule requires an arrow pot in, or next to, every room that needs the bow.
7. **Eye switches (M4b).**
   - `switch.eye?: true`: art `fix_eye`, lit only by an arrow; a sword or boomerang only clinks.
   - The solver lights it from any reachable tile on a straight 4-way line of up to 12 tiles over floor, low or pit, when the bow is owned.
8. **Pits and ghost floors (M4b).**
   - Terrains `pit` (solid, low, auto-tiled) and `ghost` (walkable, drawn as a pit).
   - A pure `ghostShown(sim)` gives the ghost tiles inside the lantern's circle (once owned) or a lit brazier's (`FIRE_RADIUS × 2`).
   - `GhostView` draws those as faint flagstones over the pit, per frame.
   - Nothing in the sim or the solver changes: a ghost floor is floor.
9. **Grave-gold and sleepers (M4b).**
   - Enemy thing `asleep?: true`: `mem.asleep`. `runEnemies` skips it, it takes no hits (i-frames held), does no harm and shows anim `sleep`. It still counts for `clear`.
   - `PropDef.wakes?: true` (`grave_gold`): lifting one wakes every sleeper on the screen (`sfx_wake`); each goes into its start state. It breaks into silver.
   - Integrity: every screen with sleepers has grave-gold.
   - The solver needs nothing new: gold can always be lifted.
10. **Mini-boss (M4b).**
    - `EnemyDef.boss.mini?: true`: the boss bar shows it. `killEnemy` kills its summons and shakes, but neither marks `bossDead` nor emits `bossDead`.
    - `bossAlive` in the solver skips it; its `needs` still gate `clear`.
11. **Enemy shots (M4b).**
    - `ShotId` gains `arrow` (4 px a tick, 90 ticks) and `axe` (flies out 120 px and back to its thrower, then gone).
    - Both use `stepSpit`'s rules (shield, sword knock-away) with their own art.
12. **Farvegr and readying (M4b).**
    - `SONGS` may defer the cost. Farvegr, sung outdoors on the overworld, starts a story `[{k:'farvegr'}]`; anywhere else it fizzles free.
    - The step lists `world.warps` in region order plus "stay", with StoryUi `{k:'warps', rows, cursor}`.
      - Confirm pays the cost, then queues a fade out, the warp to the stone's `arrive`, and a fade in.
      - Cancel ends it.
    - Command `{t:'ready', galdr}` moves a known galdr to the front of `inv.galdr`. The pause menu's items tab lists galdr rows after the food.
13. **Enemies (M4b).**
    - `bogdraugr`: keeps 64–112 px from Ask, backing off when closer; 24-tick draw; an `arrow` shot.
    - `haugvordr`: `shield`, 16 hp, 30-tick bash tell, a charge (HEAVY 4) until a wall, dazed 90 with `mem.open`; a sweep (24 tell) within 28 px.
    - `haugkonungr`: 24 hp, `mem.guard` except when stunned, gems (`mem.gems` 3), phases at 16 and 8.
      - The charge tell is 30 ticks (22 in phase 3); charges stop at walls (dazed 40).
      - An arrow hit while `mem.exposed` (the tell and the charge) breaks a gem: stunned 150, guard off.
      - Phase 2 summons two draugr on each rise and charges twice.
      - Phase 3 throws its axe between charges.
      - `needs: ['bow']`.

## Core interface changes (CLAUDE.md "plan first")

**`GameState`/`SaveData`: no change, and `SAVE_VERSION` stays 1.**
- New state lives in flags, `world.warps`, `world.opened`, `world.pieces`, `dungeons.d3`, and the arrow count.
- Fixtures `v1-m4a.json` and `v1-m4b.json` are added, and every older one must still load.

**The golden hash stays `f865b677`:** scaling is inert below two stones, sleepers and shots are absent unless authored, and the thrust and parry need their flags.

- **`Sim`/`SimRt`:** no new public methods. `storyUi()` gains the warps picker; `boss()` shows mini-bosses too.
- **`ContentDb`:** gains `lowlands`.
- **`Tuning`:** gains `stones` and `bow`; `HeroTuning` gains `thrust*`, `parryTicks` and `shootTicks`.
- **`Thing`:**
  - Adds `warp`.
  - `enemy` gains `asleep`; `switch` gains `eye`.
  - `GateArt` gains `slab`.
- **`EnemyDef`:** gains `shield`, and `boss.mini`.
- **`PropDef`:** gains `wakes`. `ItemDef` gains `fires` and `comes`.
- **`DropKind`/`DropTable`:** gain `arrows`. `ShotId` gains `arrow` and `axe`.
- **Hit tags:** gain `PIERCE`.
- **`HeroMode`:** gains `thrust` and `shoot`.
- **Story:** `Step {k:'farvegr'}`, `StoryRun.warps?` and `StoryUi {k:'warps'}`.

- **SFX:** `sfx_warp`, `sfx_thrust`, `sfx_parry`, `sfx_bow`, `sfx_arrow_hit`, `sfx_wake`, `sfx_gem`, `sfx_axe`.

## Risks
- **Runestone scaling vs old routes:** inert below two stones, so M1–M3 routes are unaffected. The M4 routes are the first to feel it.
- **The parry vs existing blocks:** it only triggers with `t_parry`, which no older state has.
- **Night-gated progress:** the watch must be reachable in every season. Styrr's wait makes the night one choice away, and the solver proofs set the flag directly (it depends on a fight at night, which the solver does not model).
- **Ghost floors and readability:** the shell draws them only in light. Without the lantern, braziers along each ghost path show the way. The playtest will judge.
- **Soft-locks:**
  - Lock C sits behind the bow's bridge, so three keys for three locks never strand Ask.
  - The solver proves every order.
- **Arrow starvation:** arrow pots next to every bow need, archers that drop arrows, and the King's summons drop arrows.
- **Scope:** 31 tasks. Each part ships alone.
- **Budget:** expect about +50–70 KB on 499.4 of 730.
