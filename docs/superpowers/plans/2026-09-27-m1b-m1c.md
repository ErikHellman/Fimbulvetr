# M1b + M1c — Raid, Myrkviðr and Rótarhellir — implementation plan

## Context

The user asked to "proceed with M2". The roadmap shows only M1a (farm days) and the world polish as done. Spec-M2 (Uppvík) builds on two milestones that don't exist yet:
- **M1b:** enemy AI, death, weather, the pause menu and Myrkviðr.
- **M1c:** dungeon 1, after which Uppvík opens.

The user chose to plan **M1b and M1c together**. When both are done, M1 plays from New Game to the Rótvættr fight in 45–60 minutes. The goal flag is `st_stone1_lit`.

Sources:
- Brief: `docs/briefs/m1.md`
- Spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-26-fimbulvetr-design.md`
- Handoff: `docs/superpowers/handoff-2026-09-26-m1a.md`
- Polish spec: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-27-world-polish-design.md`

**Decisions made with the user this session:**
- **Two parts.** M1b and M1c are separate parts of this plan. Each ends green and playable, with its own exit route test, e2e, save fixture and handoff.
- **Game over → Continue.** Ask respawns where he entered the current screen or room, with 3 hearts. Flags, keys, chests and items are kept. The room's actors reset, including the boss's health.
- **Lantern.** Its light is automatic once owned. A K/L slot is only needed to light braziers.
- **Weather.** Only the scripted raid storm (rain, lightning, thunder). Rolled weather stays `clear` until M2.
- **Dungeon rooms** sit on a per-dungeon grid and slide into each other, Zelda-style.

## Brief additions (written into `docs/briefs/m1.md` in Task 1; this approval covers them)

### The raid (night 3)
Sleeping on day 3 runs a new `raid_begins`:
- The season changes to autumn and it is 01:00.
- Ask holds the **pitchfork**, the storm is on, and a card reads "You wake to fire".

**Where it happens:**
- **Longhouse:** fire hazards and one draugr, which teaches the attack tell.
- **Farmyard:** burning gates seal the W, E and S exits. There are fightable draugr, and immortal raid trolls on patrol.

**At the gate:** a trigger at `ask_gate` runs `raid_gate`:
- Kolbeinn (cutscene-only NPC) takes Embla and the eight villagers.
- Halvar is wounded at the gate.
- A card, then morning at 07:00: `st_raid_done`, and the storm ends.

**The morning:**
- Halvar, in bed, gives the seax and shield (`st_seax_given`).
- Gyða at the hof tells the legend: `st_legend_told`, the clock policy becomes `cycling`, and `q_runestone_1` starts.
- The palisade gate on `ask_gate` opens. It is a `gate` fixture, closed while `st_legend_told` is not set.

**Rules around the raid:**
- The clock is frozen from `st_raid_begun` until `st_raid_done`, so the raid night never dawns.
- The taken villagers' `places` get `not(st_raid_begun)`.
- Optional, droppable: Ulf's lost-lamb errand on day 3 with the second Askdalr heart piece (`q_lamb`).

### Rótarhellir — D1 (grid `layout.dungeons.d1`, 3×4; a draft that the solver proves in M1c)
```
        c0                c1                  c2
r0  d1_r10 secret cache  d1_r12 Rótvættr     d1_r09 heart-piece puzzle
r1  d1_r07 boomerang     d1_r11 pre-boss     d1_r08 compass
r2  d1_r05 key 2 (clear) d1_r04 root puzzle  d1_r06 map
r3  d1_r02 vines         d1_r01 entrance     d1_r03 sap, key 1
```

**Rooms:**
- `r01` is safe. Its door leads back to `myr_roots`.
- `r02` teaches vines and the root-biter.
- `r03` teaches sap and holds key 1.
- `r04` has pushable roots. It opens the shutter to `r05`, and holds lock A to `r11` (a shortcut).
- `r05` is a clear-the-room shutter room with key 2. Lock B leads to `r07`.
- `r07` has the boomerang chest (mid-dungeon).
- Boomerang switches:
  - across the sap pit, `r07`→`r11`
  - the boss door `r11`→`r12`, which combines pushing and the boomerang
- `r09` is optional: the heart piece, with boomerang and roots.
- `r10` is optional: a dark room with braziers (lantern) and the silver cache.
- The lantern is never required.

**The boss and the ending:**
- **Rótvættr:**
  1. Stunning all three bulbs with the boomerang exposes the core; the sword hits it.
  2. The bulbs recover faster, and root-biters are summoned.
  3. Root spikes erupt under Ask.
- On death: the heart container appears, the shutters open, and the runestone lights (`st_stone1_lit`).
- Kolbeinn cameo, then a warp out to `myr_roots` and "To be continued".

**Flags:**
- M1b: `st_raid_done`, `st_seax_given`, `st_legend_told` (plus optional `q_lamb_found`, `q_lamb_done`).
- M1c: `st_d1_entered`, `st_d1_boss_dead`, `st_stone1_lit`.

**Quests:** `q_legend`, `q_runestone_1`.

## Core interface changes (CLAUDE.md "plan first")

- **`Sim`**
  - `mode` gains `'over'`.
  - New `entry: {x, y, facing}`: where the hero entered the current screen. It is set by slides, doors, warps and load, and it is hashed. **The golden hash is re-recorded once, on purpose, in Task 4, and the commit says so.**
  - New read-only methods: `weather()`, `darkness()`, `lights()`, `boss()`, `flushCommands()`.
- **`LoadedScreen`**
  - Gains `base` (the terrain-only collision grid) and `dungeon: DungeonId | null`.
  - `collision` = `base` plus the SOLID cells of closed fixtures and pushed blocks, restamped each tick. Enemies, the walker and the solver all respect it.
- **`GameState`/`SaveData`: no change, and `SAVE_VERSION` stays 1.**
  - New helper `dungeonOf(state, id)` (`src/core/state/dungeons.ts`) creates a default `DungeonState` when one is missing. This closes the "normalise `dungeons.d1`" carry-over with no migration.
  - A tooling test forbids reading `state.dungeons[` anywhere else in core.
  - Keys, maps and compasses are ItemIds (`small_key`, `big_key`, `dungeon_map`, `compass`). `giveItem` routes them into the current dungeon's state.
  - Chests and hearts use `world.opened`; pieces use `world.pieces`.
  - New `tests/content/persisted.test.ts`: an append-only list of every chest, lock, piece and heart id.
- **`ContentDb`:** gains `weather: WeatherRule[]` (`{when: Cond, kind}`; first match wins, otherwise `clear`; indoors and dungeons are always `clear`) and `freezeClock?: Cond`.
- **`WorldLayout`:** gains `dungeons?: Partial<Record<DungeonId, {cols, rows, at}>>`.
  - `LayoutIndex` gains `gridOf(id)`.
  - Each dungeon grid gets its own origin block below the pockets, and `neighbourOf` never crosses grids.
- **`ScreenDef`:** gains `dungeon?` (no clock, no weather, keys shown in the HUD) and `dark?`.
- **`Thing`**
  - The `enemy` variant gains `when?` and `onDeath?: Effect[]`, so boss flags are set by content and stay visible to the story test.
  - New fixture variants, spawned as `EntityKind 'fixture'`, with conditions re-evaluated every tick and latched state in `mem`:
    - `chest {id, at, gives, appear?, when?}`
    - `heart {id, at, appear?}`
    - `lock {id, at, w, h}`
    - `shutter {at, w, h, opens: RoomSignal, when?}`
    - `gate {at, w, h, art, closed: Cond}`
    - `fire {at, w, h, when?}`
    - `switch {at, by}`
    - `brazier {at}`
  - `RoomSignal` is `clear | switches | braziers | blocks`, derived from actors each tick. A shutter arms only once the hero's body has left it.
- **`PropDef`:** gains `pushable?` (a root block that moves one tile after 16 ticks of pushing and resets when the room is re-entered). Vines are props with `breakBy: 'sword'`.
- **`CoverDef`:** gains `hides?` and `blown?`. `COVERS` gains `leaves`.
- **Entities**
  - `EntityKind` gains `'fixture'` and `'projectile'`.
  - `HeroMode` gains `dying`, `toss` and `push`.
  - No new `Entity` fields: new per-entity data goes in `mem`.
- **`EnemyDef`:** gains `attacks?` (per FSM state: `boxes` by direction, `from`/`to` ticks, `amount`, `knock`, `tags`), `stunnable?`, `guard?`, `drops?` and `boss?: {name: L10n}`.
- **`ActorCtx`:** gains `others`, `heroFsm`, and `spawn(id, at, facing)` (projectiles and summons).
- **`Command`:** gains `equip {slot, item}`, `eat {item}` and `continue`, plus dev-only `setHp`, `god`, `weather` and `killAll`.
- **`SimEvent`:** gains `killed {id, def, x, y}`, `gameOver`, `shake {amount}` and `bossHit`.
- **`WeatherKind`:** gains `storm`, which is override-only and never rolled.
- **`Tuning`:** gains `weapons.pitchfork`, `boomerang`, `push`, `hero.dyingTicks` and `hero.tossTicks`.
- **`DevPreset`:** gains `season`, `policy`, `hp`, `maxHp`, `slots`, `pieces`, `opened` and `dungeons`.
- **No change:** `Cond`, `Effect` and script `Step`. The raid uses an existing same-screen `warp` step to spawn its enemies.

## Risks
- **Golden hash.**
  - Re-record it only in Task 4 (`Sim.entry`), and say so in the commit.
  - New per-entity data goes in `mem`, and optional hashed values default to `undefined`.
  - Never add things to `test_a`/`test_b`.
  - Each newly hashed part gets a "hash differs when X differs" test.
- **Typed records.** A new NPC, enemy, SFX, terrain or cover id must land together with its record entries (`LOOKS`, `ENEMY_DEFS`, `SFX_BANK`, `TERRAIN_ART`, `COVER_ART`), or typecheck fails.
- **Raid edge cases.**
  - The raid night dawning is handled by `freezeClock`.
  - Enemies only spawn at screen entry, so `raid_begins` ends with a same-screen `warp`.
- **Soft-locks.** Shutters arm only after the hero leaves the doorway. The solver proves the key orders.
- **Route fragility against AI.** Real fights are deterministic, so a route breaks only when tuning changes. Enemy balance is proven by per-enemy tests. The fallback is a harness `db` patch that zeroes damage.
- **E2E flakiness.**
  - Tests assert through `__fimbul` state polls and never fight in real time.
  - Lightning is shell-only; tests assert counts, not visuals.
  - WebKit isn't in this container, so CI runs it.
- **Budget.** About 405 KB of 730 KB is used; I expect about +50 KB gzipped. Phaser's particles and render textures are already in the bundle.
- **Art volume.** Enemies draw `s` and `w` only (`e` is mirrored); the draugr and Kolbeinn reuse people Looks.
- **Scope.** 45 tasks. M1b ships on its own, so stopping at any task boundary leaves a green build.
