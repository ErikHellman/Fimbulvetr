## System designs

1. **The rime melts (M6a).**
   - A `gate` thing gains `melts?: FlagId`. An Eldr bolt that reaches a closed tile of such a gate sets the flag, shakes the screen and bursts (`sfx_fire`).
   - `stepEldr` checks for a melting gate on the bolt's next box before its wall test, since a closed gate is stamped solid.
   - `hau_pass`'s rime gate gets `melts: 'st_rime_open'`, and the gorge's rows 0–1 open north to `nif_gorge`.
   - **Solver:** a melting gate opens (its flag set) once Eldr is known and one of its tiles is beside the reach.
2. **Mist regions (M6a).**
   - `ClockRules.misty?: readonly RegionId[]`: outdoors there, the fog layer is always thick (`Place.misty`), whatever the sky.
   - `ClockRules.regionWeather?`: a region's own weather table per season. Niflmýrr's has no rain (clear, wind; snow in winter), so snow can fall on top of the fog.
   - Night in a misty region is darker by `MIST_DARK` (like the storm's extra dark).
   - `Sim.fog()` reads the same `Place`. The lantern still widens the clear circle; Ljós later burns it off.
3. **Niflmýrr's screens (M6a).** Content: ten screens on grid columns 3–7, rows 0–1, entered only from the gorge; the integrity test checks seams in every season. Spawn table `niflmyrr` with night mara.
4. **Rune-staves (M6a).** An item group `STAVES` (`stave_is`, max 3) with `ItemDef.stave: GaldrId`. Readied from the menu like a galdr; the galdr button then casts its galdr for no seiðr and uses one stave. Sölvi sells `stave_is` once the pass is open.
5. **Ís (M6a for the stave, M6b for good).** `SONGS.is`: a bolt like Eldr's (no wind), frost on the first thing it meets:
   - A foe freezes for `Tuning.is.freeze` (240 ticks): no moves, no blows, open to a shattering blow (double damage). Bosses are immune.
   - Still `water` in a 3×3 patch ices over (the cover's `ice` kind, saved like a cut, in any season); `rapids` and the Gjöll never freeze.
6. **Map verses (M6a).** The skald's verses set `w_verse_*` flags. `mapModel` gains a markers layer: a screen's secret shows on the pause map once its verse is held.
7. **Ljós (M6a).** `SONGS.ljos`: a light around Ask for `Tuning.ljos.ticks` (1200), which clears fog on the screen (`Sim.fog()` → none while it burns), shows `ghost` floor (as the lantern does, wider) and reveals `mara`.
8. **The grapple chain (M6b).** A sub-item fired the way Ask faces (6 tiles):
   - It hooks a `post` fixture and pulls Ask across pits and water to the tile before it.
   - It pulls light foes (`EnemyDef.light`) and pickups to Ask.
   - It tears a shield away (`EnemyDef.shield` with `grapple: 'disarm'`).
   - **Solver:** a post in a straight line of up to 6 tiles of floor, pit or water from a reached tile adds the tile before the post.
9. **Moving platforms (M6b).** A `raft` thing rides a fixed path over water or pit; Ask standing on it moves with it. The solver treats a raft's path ends as joined.
10. **Fog rooms (M6b).** A dungeon room flag `fog: true`: the fog layer with the lantern's circle only.
11. **Thanes and captives (M6b).** Flags `q_thanes` (int 4) and `q_captives` (int 8); thane flags `st_thane_*`; captives' `st_freed_*`. Content, plus a homecoming for each captive keyed on its flag.
12. **New enemies:** `mara` (M6a), `fog_draugr` (M6a), `helhound`, `garmr`, `nastrond` (M6b). Each with a behaviour, art and telegraph test.

## Core interface changes (CLAUDE.md "plan first")

**`GameState`/`SaveData`: no change, and `SAVE_VERSION` stays 1.** New state lives in flags, items, `world.pieces`, `world.opened` and saved cover. Fixtures `v1-m6a.json` and `v1-m6b.json` are added, and every older one must still load.

**The golden hash stays unchanged:** nothing old changes before the pass, and the golden run never reaches Niflmýrr.

- **`ContentDb`:** no new tables. `ClockRules` gains the optional `misty` and `regionWeather`.
- **`Sim`:** `fog()` reads misty regions and Ljós; `lights()` gains Ljós. M6b adds `grapple()` for the view.
- **`Thing`:** `gate.melts` (M6a); `post` and `raft` (M6b).
- **`ItemDef`:** gains `stave` (M6a).
- **`EnemyDef`:** gains `light` and the grapple disarm (M6b).
- **SFX:** `sfx_melt`, `sfx_is`, `sfx_ljos` (M6a); `sfx_chain` (M6b).
