## System designs

1. **Killing frost (M9a).**
   - `ScreenDef.cold?: true` marks an outdoor screen as cold.
   - The runtime carries `coldTicks`. Each tick in a cold screen adds 1, unless Ask wears the ember byrnie (then it drains as when warm). Elsewhere it drains 4 a tick.
   - At `Tuning.hero.cold` (240 ticks, 4 s), Ask freezes: `coldBurn` hp (2, half a heart) every 60 ticks, through the same path as heat burn. God mode is spared.
   - `Sim.cold()` returns `{ now, max }` or null for the HUD, which draws a pale blue bar where the heat bar goes.
   - Never saved.
   - **Solver:** a cold screen is closed unless the state owns the ember byrnie (`inv.armor` or the flag that Sindri's forging sets). The solver assumes Ask wears what Ask owns.
2. **Glaze and sliding (M9a).**
   - Terrain `glaze` (`{ solid: false, glaze: true }`), legend char `≈`.
   - In `moveAll`, a hero on foot (`move` state) whose feet are on glaze slides: the first step onto it, or a push of the stick while standing on it, sets `mem.slide` to a direction (the dominant axis of the input). The hero's cross-axis position snaps to the tile's centre. While sliding, the input is ignored and Ask moves `Tuning.hero.slide` px (2) a tick that way.
   - A slide ends when a step moves Ask less than half the speed (a wall, a rock, a foe) or Ask's feet leave the glaze (onto ground, which they then stand on). Hurt, death, transitions and a door clear it.
   - Foes walk glaze as ground (Svellr's charge is its own behaviour).
   - **Solver:** a step onto glaze, or any step from a glaze tile, runs on in that direction while the next tile is walkable glaze. It stops on the last glaze tile before something unwalkable, or on the first walkable tile that is not glaze. A slide never crosses a screen edge, and the content test checks that no glaze touches one.
3. **Blizzards (M9a).**
   - `ClockRules.blizzard?: readonly RegionId[]`: in those regions, a snow sky is a blizzard.
   - `Sim.blizzard()` is true outdoors in such a region under snow. The view draws a white veil with a clear circle of 72 px round Ask (Ljós widens it, as in the fog).
   - The wind pushes Ask `Tuning.hero.gust` px a tick (0.25) along `windOf`, through `moveEntity`'s belt slot. A slide is not pushed.
   - **Solver:** blizzards change nothing.
4. **Light beams (M9b).**
   - New things:
     - `{ k: 'beam', id, at, dir, when? }`: a shaft of rime-light from a window. It shines while `when` holds.
     - `{ k: 'prism', id, at, turn: '/' | '\\', turns? }`: a block that turns a beam 90°. With `turns`, a sword blow or a Bragð beam turns it a quarter (it toggles `/` and `\`). Turns are kept in `rt.prisms` and reset on leaving the room.
     - `{ k: 'eye', id, at, flag }`: a crystal eye that sets `flag` for good the first time a beam or a Bragð beam hits it.
   - `systems/beams.ts` traces each lit beam every tick: from its tile, straight on, until a tile that stops it (solid, not `LOW`), the room's edge, or 60 tiles.
     - A prism turns it.
     - Ask holding the mirror turns it Ask's facing, unless Ask faces the way it travels (the mirror's back).
     - An eye takes it.
   - `Sim.beams()` returns the segments for the view, which draws them as pale lines with a glow.
   - Prisms and eyes are solid; prisms can be struck.
   - **Solver:** a beam is traced over fixed prisms and with each `turns` prism in both states. An eye whose tile it reaches sets its flag. A beam that needs the mirror is lit when the solver holds the mirror and a reachable tile lies on the beam's path with a straight line from it to the eye (a single mirror bounce). Bragð lights an eye in a straight line of up to 14 tiles from a reached tile.
5. **The ice mirror (M9b).**
   - `USES.mirror` puts the hero in a new state `mirror` while the item button stays held. Ask stands, can turn with the stick (no walking), and the shield's block still covers the front.
   - Rime bolts (`rime_bolt` projectiles from `frostvaettr` and Hrímgerðr) that reach Ask's mirror leave the way Ask faces as hero bolts. A hero bolt hurts the first foe it touches (3 damage). Hrímgerðr reads it as her stagger.
6. **Svellr and Hrímgerðr (M9b).** Behaviour machines in `core/actors/enemies/`, like Belgr and Ívaldi, with their numbers in exported consts and their tells in `setPieces`.

## Core interface changes (CLAUDE.md "plan first")

**`GameState` and `SaveData` do not change, and `SAVE_VERSION` stays 1.** Cold, slides, blizzards, beams and prism turns live on the runtime and the entities. Fixtures `v1-m9a.json` and `v1-m9b.json` are added, and every older one must still load.

**The golden hash stays unchanged:** no old screen gains glaze or cold. `dvg_ledges` gains an opening on its north edge, which the golden run never walks.

- **`ContentDb`:** no new tables.
  - `TerrainDef` gains `glaze`; terrain id `glaze` is appended.
  - `ScreenDef` gains `cold`.
  - `ClockRules` gains `blizzard`.
  - `Tuning.hero` gains `cold`, `coldBurn`, `slide` and `gust`.
- **`Sim`:** gains `cold()`, `blizzard()` and `beams()`, for the view.
- **`Thing`:** gains `beam`, `prism` and `eye`.
- **`HeroMode`:** gains `mirror`.
- **SFX:** `sfx_frost`, `sfx_slide`, `sfx_glass`, `sfx_mirror`.
- **Ids appended:** NPC `ormr`; quest `q_rime`; enemies `isvargr`, `frostvaettr`, `svellr`, `hrimgerdr`; the M9 screens, flags, scripts and pieces.
