| 7 Fishing core | Pure rules in `src/core/story/fishing.ts` (`FishRun`, `stepFishing`, `FISHING`), run by the new step `{k:'fish', float, each?}` (a blocking step like the shop; `StoryRun.fish` exists only while it runs, so the hash is untouched otherwise). Phases: idle (confirm casts, cancel puts the rod down) → cast (24) → wait (90–360, with 0–2 eight-tick nibbles; a strike spooks the fish) → bite (a 20-tick window, then missed) → reel → result (60 ticks, or a press). While reeling, holding confirm/interact/sword adds the fish's `pull` to the tension and reels 12 in; letting go takes 25 off and the fish runs its `run` back. Tension ≥1000 snaps the line; 45 ticks slack at 0, or running 400 past where it was hooked, loses it; distance 0 lands it (its silver, its `onLand`, then the step's `each`). Surges come every 40–100 ticks for 20: a held line strains by the fish's `surge` extra (the tell: the float jerks). **Ruling (found by the test):** at first a surge also doubled the fish's run on a slack line, and Gamli could not be landed by any policy. Now a surge only punishes holding, the reel is 12, and a fish can be landed while `run × pull < reel × slack` (300). Randomness comes from `state.rng`, drawn only while fishing, so replays stay exact; the clock is frozen, as in any story. `content/fish.ts`: perch, pike, bream, trout (mornings and evenings), eel (nights), burbot (winter evenings and nights), salmon, and Gamli (autumn dawn and dusk, 1,600 out, pull 14, run 9, surge 30; sets `q_fish_gamli`). `ContentDb.fish`; `FishId`. `StoryUi {k:'fish', …}` (phase, float, nibble, bite, tension and its band, distance, surging, fish, result); the hook reports the phase. The hero has a `fish` pose (a rod held out, the tip dipping). New effect `{k:'piece', id}`: a piece of heart handed over, through `grantPiece` (moved from `collectPickups` into `story/effects.ts`, shared by both). Flags `q_fish_caught` (int, 99) and `q_fish_gamli`. SFX `sfx_bite`, `sfx_reel`, `sfx_snap`, `sfx_splash`. Tests: `tests/sim/fishing.test.ts` (rod out and put down; spooked; missed; landed with silver and the count; slipped; species by season and part of the day; Gamli landed by a steady hand, snapped by hauling, lost by slacking; a salmon snaps when hauled; exact replays; a handed piece counts once). |
