# Ledger — M7a Sævatn and the Refuge

Plan: `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-04-m7.md` (Part 1). Brief: `docs/briefs/m7.md`, approved 2026-10-04. Branch: `claude/project-thread-hru6i7`, continuing after M6b (ea74299). Baseline: `pnpm check` green, 2676 tests; golden hash `f865b677`; budget 568.5 KB gz of 730.

## System details pinned before building

- **Deep water.** `TerrainDef.swim: true` on `water`, `spring` and the current terrains. `buildCollision` adds the flag bit `DEEP` (64). `rapids`, `blackwater`, `sap` and `pit` stay unswimmable, so Helgrind and Niflmýrr keep their puzzles.
- **Who swims.** With `sealskin` owned, `heroSolidAt` opens a tile that has `DEEP` and still has `LOW`. Ice cover and a resting raft's deck take `LOW` off, so they stay ground to walk on. Foes, critters and props keep treating water as a wall.
- **The hero machine.** New states `swim` and `dive`. `move` (and `shield`) turn into `swim` once Ask's feet are on open deep water; `swim` turns back into `move` on footing. Swimming is steered at `swimSpeed` (1.05, 70% of 1.5), with no sword, shield, item, galdr or interact.
- **Diving.** Roll in `swim` (cooldown permitting) dives for `diveTicks` (120). `hurtHero` ignores every blow while Ask dives. A diver takes the `sunk` chest or piece under Ask's feet. The dive ends in `swim`, or in `move` if Ask came up on footing; the roll cooldown follows.
- **Currents.** `TerrainDef.current: Dir4` pushes a swimmer by `current` (0.6 px a tick) in that direction, added to their own steering. `strong: true` pushes by `strongCurrent` (2.2) instead, and never pushes a diver. Terrains: `current_n/e/s/w` (legend `/ > | <`) and `surge_n/e/s/w` (legend `[ } ] {`). None of them freeze.
- **Sunk things.** `chest.sunk` and `piece.sunk`: drawn as a ripple (the fixture art `fix_ripple`), never opened by interact or picked up by walking; a dive with feet on its tile takes it.
- **Art.** Each hero kit gains `swim` (4 frames, all facings: the pose cut off at the waterline over a ripple ring) and `dive` (2 frames, `s`: rings and bubbles).
- **Sounds.** `sfx_splash` (into the water; it already existed) and `sfx_dive` (new).
- **Solver.** With `sealskin`, tiles that are `DEEP` and `LOW` join the floor (a strong current is crossed by diving). A sunk chest or piece is taken once its tile is reached by swimming.
- **Golden hash.** The golden run never owns the seal-skin, so it does not change.
