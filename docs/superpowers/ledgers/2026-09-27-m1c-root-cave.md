| Task | Notes and rulings |
| --- | --- |
| 1 dungeonOf | `src/core/state/dungeons.ts`: `emptyDungeon` and `dungeonOf` (creates a missing entry on first use). `newGame` and `applyPreset` use them. A tooling test forbids indexing `.dungeons[` anywhere else in core. Closes the M0 carry-over with no migration and no save-format change. |
| 2 Dungeon grids | `WorldLayout.dungeons` (one `ScreenGrid` per dungeon); `indexLayout` keys cells by grid, places each dungeon grid in its own origin block below the pockets and adds `gridOf`; `neighbourOf` never crosses grids, so rooms slide into each other. `ScreenDef.dungeon`: no clock, no weather, no night (`darkness` counts a room as sheltered unless `dark`); the shell grades caves with a fixed cool light. Integrity: rooms on a grid carry its `dungeon` and every grid is entered by a door from outside; seams already run per grid. Golden unchanged. |
| 3 Chests and heart container | `chest` things are fixtures (one per chest, `fix_chest` closed/open), solid once in sight and opened with interact: saved in `world.opened`, the gift handed over, `sfx_chest` and the item's `found` line as a story say. `heart` things are pickups saved in `world.opened`. Both wait hidden (`mem.wait`) until `when` holds and the room gives its `appear` signal (`RoomSignal` `clear` so far, in `systems/rooms.ts`); a chest never appears on top of Ask, and appearing chimes `sfx_secret`. `DUNGEON_ITEMS` (small_key, big_key, dungeon_map, compass) are ItemIds whose `ItemDef.dungeon` routes them into `dungeonOf` the current room's dungeon, never the bag. `ItemDef.hearts` makes a heart container add a heart and refill. `HEART`/`MAX_HP` moved to effects.ts. Slides wait while a story runs (a pickup can start one mid-tick). Golden unchanged. |
| 4 Locks, shutters, switches, braziers | `fixtures.ts` now runs on a per-kind table (`solid`: never/on/always, on/off anims). `lock`: walking into it (or interact) with a small key spends the key and saves the id in the dungeon's `doors`, so the same id is open from both rooms. `shutter`: arms once Ask's body is clear of its rectangle (never during spawn, before Ask is placed), closes, and opens for good when its `opens` signal first holds; with an `id` the opening is saved in `doors`; open while `when` fails. `switch`: lit by a sword strike (`strikeSwitch` is shared with the boomerang). `brazier`: lit from the lantern in a slot (`USES.lantern`), glows in `lights()`. `RoomSignal` gains `switches` and `braziers` (every one lit, at least one). SFX: sfx_unlock, sfx_shutter, sfx_switch. Golden unchanged. |
| 5 Pushable roots and vines | Props `root_block` (`pushable`) and `vines` (`breakBy: 'sword'`), both `PropDef.wall`: their tiles are stamped into collision (`wallTiles`), so enemies are stopped too, and breaking a vine restamps. `pushBlocks` (after movement) counts steady ticks of walking straight into a block in `hero.mem.push`; at `Tuning.push.ticks` (16) the block slides a tile over `push.slideTicks` (16) if the tile beyond is on screen, not solid and empty, then counts as `moved`. Blocks respawn in place on re-entry. Ruling: no `push` HeroMode after all; the hero stays in `move` and shows the `push` anim while `mem.push` > 0 (art in Task 12). `RoomSignal` gains `blocks` (every pushable moved). Placeholder sprites for both props in the new `src/art/sprites/cave.ts` (the art test demands them now). SFX: sfx_push. Golden unchanged. |
| 6 Boomerang | `systems/projectiles.ts`: `EntityKind 'projectile'`; `throwBoomerang` (from `USES.boomerang`; `ItemUse` now gets the input) throws eight ways (steering, else facing), one at a time, and puts the hero in the new `toss` state (`hero.tossTicks` 10). Flight (`Tuning.boomerang` speed 3, range 112): out until range, a wall or a strike, then back to Ask's hand through anything. It stuns (0-damage hit with the STUN tag; `sfx_stun`, or a clink on the unstunnable), lights switches (`strikeSwitch`), blows away `blown` cover only (`blowCover`; `cutBox` takes a kind filter) and fetches the first visible pickup it touches, dropped at Ask's feet on the catch. `TerrainDef.low` / collision `LOW`: solid underfoot but open above; water is low, so the boomerang crosses moats. Drawn `FLY_Z` px up with a shadow (art in Task 12). SFX: sfx_boomerang, sfx_stun. Golden unchanged. |
| 7 Root-biter | `root_biter.ts`: buried (untouchable, wakes when Ask's feet are within 48 px) → emerge (the 24-tick tell, anim `tell`) → bite (window 0–5, 2 damage) → up for 60 ticks, open to the blade → retract → buried with a 60-tick rest; rears again at once if Ask is still within 26 px. Never moves, no contact damage, stunnable 150 (the boomerang holds it up), 4 hp. Also fixed: the draugr's `rise` pose never showed, because a start state's `enter` does not run at spawn; start states now set their pose in `tick`. Art in Task 12. Golden unchanged. |
| 8 Boss framework | `Sim.boss()` returns the first live enemy whose def has `boss` as a `BossView` (name, hp, maxHp, `mem.phase`), else null. `killEnemy` on a boss kills its summons, marks `dungeonOf(...).bossDead` and emits `shake` and `bossDead`; a boss thing never spawns again in a dungeon whose boss is dead. The heart and the shutters need nothing new: `heart` `when` the boss flag set by the enemy thing's `onDeath`, shutters `opens: 'clear'`. New SimEvents: `shake {amount}` (PlayScene shakes the camera when the setting allows) and `bossDead`. Ruling: no `bossHit` event; the shell can match `hit` targets against the boss. Golden unchanged. |
| 9 Rótvættr | `rotvaettr.ts`: the core (24 hp, boss, solid, 2 contact damage) summons three `rot_bulb`s on its first tick at fixed offsets (the room puts them across sap). It stays guarded (`mem.guard`) until every bulb is stunned at once, then opens for 150 ticks; once shut it waits for a bulb to wake (`mem.armed`) before it can open again. At 16 and 8 hp it roars (`sfx_boss_roar`, shake) into phases 1 and 2. Bulbs (immortal, stunnable) keep their stun length in `mem.stunFor` from the core's phase: 400/300/240 ticks; `damageActor` honours `stunFor`. Phase 1+: a root-biter every 240 ticks (at most 2, spots picked with the sim RNG). Phase 2: a `root_spike` under Ask every 100 ticks: 24-tick crack (tell), erupt window 0–8 (2 damage, pierces the shield), sink, gone. Boss hits sound `sfx_boss_hit`. Fixed: a guarded clink knocked back even immovable targets; it now honours `knockResist`. Golden unchanged. |
| 10 Dungeon map model | `dungeonMap` in `world/mapModel.ts`: every room of a dungeon's grid in reading order, `shown` when visited or once the map is found, `here`, and with the compass `boss` (a living boss's room, from its enemy things) and `chest` (any chest not in `world.opened`, hidden ones too); plus keys, big key, map and compass for the HUD. Null for a dungeon without a floor. New `peekDungeon` reads dungeon state without creating it, so drawing the menu never changes the save or the hash. |
