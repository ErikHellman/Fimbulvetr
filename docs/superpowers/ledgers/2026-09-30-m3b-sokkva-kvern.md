| 2 Ammo | `ItemDef.ammo {bag, step}` and `itemMax(defs, have, id)` (`core/items/defs.ts`): bombs carry 10, plus 10 per `bomb_bag` (up to 30). `giveItem` and the shop's refusal use `itemMax`. `take` leaves ammunition at 0, still in its slot; `equip` lets ammunition sit in a slot at 0. | 3 Bombs and the blast | `systems/bombs.ts`: `placeBomb` (the bombs' item key) sets prop `bomb` 12 px ahead of Ask (at their feet if a wall is in the way), at most 2 alight (`BOMB.most`), takes one, and puts Ask in `toss`; with none it fizzles. `stepBombs` runs after `stepProps` and returns at once with no bomb out (golden hash unchanged). `PropDef.fuse` 96 ticks, blinking for the last 32; a lit bomb is lifted and thrown like any prop, and one knocked out of Ask's hands falls at their feet still burning (other props still break). `blast(rt, pos)`: a 40×40 box; foes take 8 (`force`, HEAVY), Ask 4 (`PIERCE_SHIELD`), other bombs go off 2 ticks later, props with `PropDef.blast` break (pot, stone, rock, `bomb_pot`; not the pail), switches are struck, cracks open, and cover a blade cuts plus drifts (`CoverDef.blasts`) is torn up and saved. New event `blast`, SFX `sfx_bomb`, `sfx_fuse`; props `bomb`, `bomb_pot` (loot `bombs`); art in `art/sprites/sokkva.ts` (drawn now, not in b9, since every prop needs an idle frame). Test: `tests/sim/bombs.test.ts`. |
| 4 Cracks and the mud-crab | Thing `crack {id, at, w, h, art}` (art `wall` or `rock`): one fixture per tile (`fix_crack_wall`/`fix_crack_rock`, closed/open), solid until its id is in `world.opened`; `openCracks(rt, box)` opens every crack the blast touches (`sfx_secret`). Crab art drawn now as well. Tests in `bombs.test.ts`. |
| 5 Water levels | Pure `core/world/water.ts`: `TerrainDef.rise` (`floods` or `floats`, 1 or 2), `riseFooting(rise, level)` and `levelPassage(grid, terrain, level)`. New terrains `sluice` (legend `1`, floods at 1), `sluice_hi` (`2`, floods at 2), `race` (`3`, planks float at 1) and `race_hi` (`4`, float at 2); each is drawn as at level 0 (dry flagstones, planks on the channel bottom), and the water overlay comes with the shell views (b11). `ScreenDef.water?: FlagId` names the level flag (`w_d2_level`, int 0–2). `LoadedScreen.level` is set only on screens with water, and `stampCollision` stamps the rising tiles from it before ice, floods and bridges. Thing `wheel {at, level}` (fixture `fix_wheel`, on/off, always solid; the solver treats it as a block): a sword, boomerang or blast strike sets the flag, `sfx_wheel`; it refuses with `sfx_block` when the footing under Ask would change (`footingHolds`). `systems/water.ts` `refreshWater` runs in play and in story before `refreshFixtures` and returns at once unless the flag differs from the stamped level; on a change it restamps, drowns walking foes left in water (not fliers, swimmers or the immortal) as kills, sinks resting props and pickups (`sfx_splash`), and emits `coverChanged` and `sfx_water`. Test: `tests/unit/core/water.test.ts`, `tests/sim/water.test.ts`. |
| 6 Big lock | `lock.big?: true` (the boss door, art `fix_biglock`): it opens for good when Ask walks into it holding the dungeon's big key, which is kept; small keys are turned away. The solver never branches on it: a big lock opens in `gather` as soon as the big key is held and the door is beside the reach. Tests in `tests/sim/rooms.test.ts`. |
| 7 Solver levels | `SolveOptions.within` (only these screens; edges, doors and warps out of them lead nowhere) and `levels`. Ruling: the level search is opt-in, not always on. With it, the reach is a (tile × level) graph (`tile + level × span`) over the one water flag in `within`; from a tile where a wheel can be struck (beside it, or along a boomerang line of up to 7 tiles) Ask may move to that wheel's level if the tile's footing is the same at both (the sim's refusal). Without it, screens with water stay at the state's level and wheels only block, so whole-world solves cost what they did (D2 is a dead end off `myl_mill`, so leaving its levels out cannot open anything elsewhere). The stranded check runs on the same graph and reports a tile when any level of it cannot get back. Cracks and big locks are in the graph as in tasks 4 and 6. Tests: `tests/content/solver_levels.test.ts` (a one-room fixture: a wheel floats planks to a chest only when searching levels; a wheel that floods the only way back strands Ask, and a second wheel on the far side cures it; the refusal; cracks need bombs; the big lock needs the big key). |
| 8 Lindormr | `actors/enemies/lindormr.ts`: `lindormr` (24 hp, phases at 16 and 8, `needs: ['bombs']`, not solid, no touch damage) and `lind_mound` (1 hp, solid, `guard` + `cracks: 'force'`, always drops bombs). On waking it raises four mounds (±112, −48/+64 px from its spot; their ids and regrowth timers live in its `mem`) and hides in the first. Hidden or rippling it is untouchable (i-frames kept up); its mound bubbles (a mound reads Lindormr's `in` and `decoy` from `others`). Every 120 ticks it rears: a 24-tick tell (`tell`), then a spit. Blowing up the mound it hides in (hidden or rearing) flushes it out: 150 ticks `flushed`, i-frames cleared, open to the blade and to blasts; then it burrows (30 ticks) into the nearest standing mound, and with none standing it waits for one to grow back. A blown mound regrows after 240 ticks. Phase 2 (≤ 16): a second spit 12 ticks after the first, then a 40-tick ripple to another mound, and a decoy mound bubbles too. Phase 3 (≤ 8): after the flush it coils (30 ticks, `coil`) and charges in a locked line at 3 px a tick (4, HEAVY) until it hits a wall, which dazes it for 90 ticks, open again. A phase change during `flushed` or `dazed` roars (50 ticks). Its death takes the mounds (summons). Art drawn now in `sokkva.ts`. Ruling: the shared telegraph test (`enemies_m1b.test.ts`) lists Lindormr as a set piece, since its only attack window, the charge, needs mounds and the last phase; `lindormr.test.ts` times the coil. Test: `tests/sim/lindormr.test.ts`. |
