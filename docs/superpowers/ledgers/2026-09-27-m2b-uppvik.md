New Effect `{k:"seidr", n}` (≤0 fills). Seiðr jars: `DropKind` `seidr`, optional `DropTable.seidr` weight (tables without it draw exactly as before), a jar gives 2 (`SEIDR_JAR`), art `pickup_seidr` (a corked blue jar, bobbing). The pause menu’s list shows green mead too. Test `tests/sim/mead.test.ts`. Golden unchanged. |
