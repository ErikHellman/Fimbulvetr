| 1 Ledger | This ledger. Carry-overs from M2a: none open. |
| 2 Seiðr, mead and purse | `ItemDef` gains `seidr`, `horn`, `maxSeidr`, `purse`. Mead: red heals every heart, green fills the seiðr bar, blue both (`HEAL`/`SEIDR` in `content/items.ts`); all three live in horns (`horn: true`), so `giveItem` adds only as many as `hornsFree` allows (a full set of horns refuses more). `eat` drinks too (refused when it would restore nothing) with the new `sfx_drink`. `giveItem("purse")` sets `hero.purse` to the count (300, then 999); `seidr_upgrade` adds 5 to the bar (to `MAX_SEIDR` 30) and fills it. New Effect `{k:"seidr", n}` (≤0 fills). Seiðr jars: `DropKind` `seidr`, optional `DropTable.seidr` weight (tables without it draw exactly as before), a jar gives 2 (`SEIDR_JAR`), art `pickup_seidr` (a corked blue jar, bobbing). The pause menu’s list shows green mead too. Test `tests/sim/mead.test.ts`. Golden unchanged. |
| 3 Gear | `Tuning.armor` (share of each blow taken off: tunic 0, byrnie 0.25, ember byrnie 0.4, runeplate 0.5); `hurtHero` deals `max(1, amount − round(amount × reduce))`, so the tunic changes nothing and a blow always lands. `Tuning.weapons.uppvik_sword`: combo 3/3/6, spin 6, a little more reach than the seax (which keeps the default 2/2/4). New Effects `{k:"armor", id}` and `{k:"learn", galdr}` (once). Rings are left for later milestones (none is found in M2). Test `tests/sim/gear.test.ts`. Golden unchanged. |
| 4 Shop wares | `StockEntry` is now `Ware & {price, n?, when?}`, a ware being `{item}`, `{weapon}`, `{armor}` or `{galdr}` (existing stock compiles unchanged). `buyRow(rt, shop, index)` serves the shop screen; `buy` (the dev command) still takes an item. Refusals: a weapon in hand, armour that already takes as much off a blow (compared by `Tuning.armor.reduce`, since core may not read the id order from content), a known galdr, an item at its max, mead without an empty horn (`full`). Shop rows carry `ware` (and `item` when the ware is one, so older tests still match). Shell: `shell/ui/wareText.ts` names wares for the shop panel and the hook. Tests in `tests/sim/shop.test.ts`. Golden unchanged. |
| 5 Fire | `CoverDef.burns` (tall grass, leaves). `CoverGrid.burn` (ticks left per tile, never saved) and a `burning` count so an idle screen costs nothing. `systems/fire.ts`: `ignite(rt, tx, ty)` catches standing burnable cover once; `stepFire` counts down, spreads when a tile has `spreadAt` ticks left — one step downwind (both sides too for a diagonal wind), or in calm air to each side on a hashed one-in-three chance (seed, play tick, tile) — never in the rain, and a burnt-out tile becomes cleared and saved (no cut sound). What stands in the flames is scorched: Ask through the shield (`hurtHero` now takes any `{pos, faction}` source), foes with element `fire` at most every 30 ticks (immortal ones shrug it off). `Tuning.fire` (burn 90 ticks, spread at 50 left, 2 damage). New `EnemyDef.weak` doubles an element’s damage in `damageActor`. Burning tiles glow in the dark (`fireLights`, at most a dozen). `raining()` moved to `systems/weather.ts` (fixtures use it too). The hash gains `fire` only while something burns. Test `tests/sim/fire.test.ts` (burns out and saves, not twice, not in rain, downwind, calm spread repeats, scorches Ask and a vargr, hash). Golden unchanged. |
| 6 Galdr and Eldr | `ContentDb.galdr` (`GALDR_DEFS`: name and seiðr cost). `castGaldr` (`systems/galdr.ts`, after the item slots): the galdr button in `move` or `shield` sings the first galdr known — the only one in M2 — paying its cost from `hero.seidr`, or plays `sfx_fizzle` without enough. Hero state `cast` (`Tuning.hero.castTicks` 14, anim `cast`: hands raised, then flung forward). `systems/eldr.ts`: the bolt flies eight ways like the boomerang (steering or facing), `Tuning.eldr` speed 4, range 176, plus the wind; the first thing it touches takes the fire and ends it: a foe (damage 4, element fire, halved in the rain), a cold brazier (lit), a burnable prop (the new `bramble`, a wall that only fire clears; `PropDef.burns`, `burnProp`), burnable cover (set alight) or drifts and ice (`CoverDef.melts`: a 3×3 patch cleared and saved, ice never under anyone’s feet, collision restamped). Walls stop it; water does not. Draugr are `weak` to fire. Art: hero `cast` in every kit, `fx_eldr` (a flickering fireball), `prop_bramble`; SFX `sfx_eldr`, `sfx_fizzle`. Test `tests/sim/galdr.test.ts`. Golden unchanged. |
