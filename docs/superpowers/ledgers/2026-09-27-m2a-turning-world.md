| 3 Sky and wind | New `systems/weather.ts`: `skyOf` (dev override → story rules, evaluated without weather so none can recurse → the region roll `weatherAt` with `seasonAt` when `rolled` → clear), `windOf` and `outdoors`. `Sim.weather()` is the sky outdoors and clear indoors/underground; new `Sim.sky()` (indoors too) and `Sim.wind()`. `windAt(seed, day, region, kind)` in `clock/weather.ts`: one of eight hashed directions all day (a constant unit-vector table, no trig), strength per kind (`WIND_STRENGTH`: wind 0.5, storm 0.8, snow 0.2, rain 0.15). New Cond `{k:"weather", is}` read through a lazy `CondCtx.weather` thunk (missing = clear); `condCtx` supplies it. `SimRt.weatherOverride`. Golden unchanged. |
| 4 Fog and rain | Fog is a separate layer from the dark: `fogOf(place)` in `world/light.ts` (outdoors in fog only, `FOG_THICK` 0.8) and `Sim.fog()` → `{amount, r}`, the clear circle around Ask of `FOG_RADIUS` 80 px (5 tiles) or `LANTERN_FOG_RADIUS` 112 with the lantern. Fog does not darken the day. `lights()` also answers in fog (fires and braziers carve it too). Rain and storms outdoors put out every lit brazier (`douseBraziers` in `refreshFixtures`; they stay out afterwards) and the lantern cannot light one there. Every D1 brazier is underground, so nothing changes in M1. Golden unchanged. |
| 5 Derived cover | `COVERS` gains `snow`, `drift`, `mud`, `ice` (appended). `CoverDef` gains `grows {on, by?}` (grows by itself outdoors on those terrains, with `by` only next to one of them, 8-way), `wet`, `cut: false`, `walk` and `cloak`. `buildCover` takes an optional `derive {terrain, outdoor, wet}`: the map’s own in-season cover first, else the first growing kind in registry order; `CoverGrid.wet`. Content: snow (winter, 0.7, over grass/path/field/yard/mound), drift (winter, 0.5, map char `^` over grass, uncuttable), mud (spring, 0.75, beside water or ford, only on a wet day, uncuttable), ice (winter, on water, walkable, uncuttable). `wetDay` = the region’s season is spring and its sky at 00:00 today is not clear (stable all day; the override counts at once). `coverFor` uses `seasonAt` (Hrímfjöll stays winter). `refreshCover` rebuilds on an epoch or wet change, restamps collision and keeps thawing ice under Ask’s feet. `cutCover` skips `cut: false`; `coverSpeed(rt, e, x, y)` halves cloak slowdown for the hero with `winter_cloak`. `stampCollision` takes SOLID and LOW off standing ice. New items (appended lists, so `ITEMS` only grows): `KEEPSAKES` horn (max 4), winter_cloak; `QUEST_ITEMS` charred_stave, fen_moss (max 9). `SimRt.terrainOf`. Placeholder cover art for all four (snow and ice as blankets with punched holes so a field never reads as a grid). Golden unchanged. |
