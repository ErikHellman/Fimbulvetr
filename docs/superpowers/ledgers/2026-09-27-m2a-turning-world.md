| 4 Fog and rain | Fog is a separate layer from the dark: `fogOf(place)` in `world/light.ts` (outdoors in fog only, `FOG_THICK` 0.8) and `Sim.fog()` → `{amount, r}`, the clear circle around Ask of `FOG_RADIUS` 80 px (5 tiles) or `LANTERN_FOG_RADIUS` 112 with the lantern. Fog does not darken the day. `lights()` also answers in fog (fires and braziers carve it too). Rain and storms outdoors put out every lit brazier (`douseBraziers` in `refreshFixtures`; they stay out afterwards) and the lantern cannot light one there. Every D1 brazier is underground, so nothing changes in M1. Golden unchanged. |
| 5 Derived cover | `COVERS` gains `snow`, `drift`, `mud`, `ice` (appended). `CoverDef` gains `grows {on, by?}` (grows by itself outdoors on those terrains, with `by` only next to one of them, 8-way), `wet`, `cut: false`, `walk` and `cloak`. `buildCover` takes an optional `derive {terrain, outdoor, wet}`: the map’s own in-season cover first, else the first growing kind in registry order; `CoverGrid.wet`.
