| 1 Ledger | This ledger. Carry-overs from M4a (see its handoff): the barrow's door things behind its slab gate, the great cairn's doorway and eye, the watchtower's bridge and eye, arrows at Geirmundr's. |
| 2 Bow and arrows | `arrows` is ammunition (`ammo {bag: 'quiver', step: 20}`, base 30, so 30/50/70). `ItemDef.fires` (the bow fires arrows; the HUD shows that count in the bow's slot) and `ItemDef.comes` (giving the bow fills 30 arrows). `USES.bow` → `shootArrow`: an `arrow` projectile the way Ask faces (4 ways), one arrow taken, hero state `shoot` (anim `bow`, `shootTicks` 14), `sfx_bow`; with none it clicks (`sfx_fizzle`). `stepArrow` (`Tuning.bow`: 5 px a tick, 72 ticks, 4 damage): it crosses water and low ground, dies on walls, strikes the first foe through `damageActor` with the new hit tag `ARROW` (32), and lights switches, eyes included. Ruling: a switch is solid, so the arrow checks for one on its next box before the wall check. Eye switches: `switch.eye` (art `fix_eye`) light only for an arrow (`strikeSwitch(rt, box, arrow)`); the sword clinks once per swing and the boomerang clinks and turns back (`eyeAt`). Drops: `DropKind 'arrows'` (`DropTable.arrows`), only while the bow is owned, a bundle of 5; prop `arrow_pot` (breaks to the blade, spills arrows). SFX `sfx_bow`, `sfx_wake`, `sfx_gem`, `sfx_axe` added together. Art: icons for the bow, arrows and quiver, `pickup_arrows`, `fx_arrow` by facing, `prop_arrow_pot`, `fix_eye`, the hero's `bow` anim for every kit. Hook `ammo()` gains the arrows. Test: `tests/sim/bow.test.ts`. |
| 3 Pits and ghost floors | Terrains `crypt_floor` (`7`), `crypt_wall` (`8`, auto-tiled), `pit` (`0`: solid and low, so arrows and the boomerang cross it and nobody falls) and `ghost` (`9`: plain floor, painted exactly as a pit in the same auto-tile group). Pure `core/world/ghost.ts` `shownGhosts`, read through `Sim.ghosts()`: the ghost tiles within 40 px of Ask when the lantern is owned, or 56 px of a burning brazier. The sim and the solver never care: a ghost floor is floor. Test: `tests/sim/barrow.test.ts`. |
| 4 Grave-gold and sleepers | Enemy thing `asleep`: spawned with `mem.asleep` and anim `sleep`; `runEnemies` holds it still with i-frames up, `resolveAttacks` skips it and `damageActor` ignores every hit on it; it still counts against `clear`. `PropDef.wakes` (prop `grave_gold`: liftable, fragile, three silver as loot): lifting it wakes every sleeper on the screen (`sfx_wake`), each into its start state (a draugr or wight rises). The solver needs nothing new (gold can always be lifted). Integrity: sleepers only where grave-gold lies. Art: `sleep` for the draugr and the barrow-wight (sunk into the ground), `prop_grave_gold`. Tests in `barrow.test.ts`. |
| 5 Mini-boss, shots, the archer and the warden | `boss.mini`: the boss bar shows it (`Sim.boss()`), its death takes its summons and shakes, but neither marks the dungeon's boss dead nor emits `bossDead`, and a dead dungeon boss does not keep it from spawning; the solver's `bossAlive` and boss gathering skip it (its `needs` still gate `clear`). Enemy shots are one table (`SHOTS`: `spit`, `arrow`, `axe`, each with art, speed, life, blow and height) through one `stepShot`; an axe (`out` 120 px) turns back at its reach, at a wall or when the sword strikes it, and flies through anything to its thrower (`ActorCtx.shoot`'s new `owner`), gone when caught or when the thrower is. The hero's arrows keep their own `stepArrow` (same def, hero faction). `bogdraugr` (`archer.ts`): keeps 64–112 px from Ask (backing off, and shooting anyway when backed against a wall), 24-tick draw (`tell`), an `arrow` shot; 4 hp, weak to fire, drops arrows. `haugvordr` (`warden.ts`, mini-boss, 16 hp, `shield`, solid): stands until Ask is within 112 px; close in, a 24-tick tell and a HEAVY sweep; further off, a 30-tick brace and a HEAVY bash in a straight line until a wall (a shake), which dazes it for 90 ticks with its guard down (`mem.open`), as the sweep and its recovery do. It stays dead through content (`when` not `st_d3_warden`, `onDeath` sets it). Art: `enemy_bogdraugr` (a draugr with a bow), `enemy_haugvordr` (the wight at 3/2 size), `fx_axe`. Tests: `enemies_m4.test.ts` (the archer's distance, tell and the shield; the axe's return), `warden.test.ts`. |
| 6 The Haugbúi King | `actors/enemies/king.ts` (`haugkonungr`, 24 hp, phases at 16 and 8, `needs: ['bow']`, solid, no drops). New `EnemyDef.struckBy` (hit tags): while the behaviour holds `mem.exposed`, such a hit sets `mem.struck` (`sfx_gem`) instead of clinking off, checked in `damageActor` before the guard. He sits on his throne until Ask is within 128 px, roars, then stalks with `mem.guard` up (mail turns every blow): a 24-tick tell and a HEAVY sweep when close; every 150 ticks, when Ask is further than 48 px, he lowers his head with his crown ablaze (`lower`, 30 ticks; 22 in the last phase; `exposed`) and charges in a locked line (HEAVY) until a wall, which only dazes him (40 ticks). An arrow in the crown in `lower` or `charge` fells him (`fallen`, 150 ticks, guard down); a phase change gets him up at once, roaring. Phase 2: each time he rises two draugr archers climb out of the floor (his summons; they drop arrows, so the fight never starves), and a second charge follows the first after a 20-tick tell. Phase 3: every 110 ticks, when Ask is further than 40 px, a 24-tick wind-up and the returning `axe`. Rulings: the summons are archers rather than plain draugr, to feed the bow; gems are only drawn (blazing on `lower`/`charge`), not counted. Art: `enemy_haugkonungr` (the wight grown half again, a gold crown with three gems, six rows of headroom). Test: `tests/sim/king.test.ts`. |
| 7 Farvegr and readying | Command `ready {galdr}` moves a known galdr to the front of `inv.galdr` (the one the button sings); unknown ones are refused. Songs now answer `pay`, `later` or `fizzle`: Eldr pays as before; Farvegr, sung on an overworld screen outdoors with the seiðr for it, starts the story step `{k:'farvegr'}` and pays nothing yet, and anywhere else (indoors, underground) fizzles for free. The step (`StoryRun.warps`, absent otherwise; StoryUi `{k:'warps', rows, cursor}`) lists `world.warps` in the order the stones were woken, then "stay"; confirm on a stone takes the seiðr, plays `sfx_warp` and queues a fade out, a warp to the stone's `arrive` (facing south) and a fade in; cancel or "stay" ends it free. Ruling: woken order rather than region order (the brief said region order), since the save keeps that order and it reads as "the stones you have found". Shell: `shell/ui/warpText.ts` (tested) draws the picker in the shop's panel; the pause menu's items tab lists the galdr known after the food (`MenuItem` kind `galdr`, "(ready)" on the first) and confirm readies one (`MenuAction` `ready`); icon `galdr_farvegr`. The hook's `story()` reports the picker. Test: `tests/sim/farvegr.test.ts`, `pauseMenu.test.ts`, `warpText.test.ts`. |
| 8 Solver | `shootable`: once the bow is owned (arrows never run out for good: pots and archers refill them), a tile can be shot from any reachable tile on a straight four-way line of up to 12 tiles over floor or low ground (water, pits). `strikable` decides a switch: an eye only by an arrow; a plain one beside the reach, by boomerang or by arrow.
