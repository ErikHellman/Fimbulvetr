### What the code check turned up (it shapes the design)
- **Ids already there:** region `hrimfjoll` (always winter in `content/clock.ts`), dungeon `d7`, sub-item `mirror`, trade item `trade_lens`, ring `ring_beacon`, NPCs `asa` and `bjarni`, flags `q_thanes` (max 4) and `q_captives` (max 8).
- **Bragð is already in the game.** Styrr's last duel at Haugar teaches it (M5, `scripts/pass.ts`). The spec's "Bragð duel" is done; M9 gives the beam a new use instead: a Bragð beam lights a crystal eye from afar.
- **The grid:** columns 9–15 of rows 0–1 are empty. `dvg_ledges` (9,2) has a sign for the high road and a wall along its north edge.
- **Heat:** `systems/heat.ts` builds heat in `hot` screens; the ember byrnie lengthens it. Killing frost is its mirror image and shares the HUD slot.
- **Weather:** `snow` is a rolled kind, and Hrímfjöll's winter table rolls it 40% of days. There is no blizzard kind.
- **Ice:** cover `ice` (winter water) and `is_ice` (the song) are walkable floors with no slide.
- **Save:** `SAVE_VERSION` stays 1. Every M9 id is appended. Cold, slides, beams and prism turns live on the runtime and entities, never in `GameState`. `GameState`, `SaveData`, `Sim` and `ContentDb` keep their shape; the new fields are append-only and listed in the plan first.

### Decisions (Claude's calls, taken)
- **Where Hrímfjöll sits:** grid columns 9–13, rows 0–1. The way in is `dvg_ledges`' north edge, where the high road climbs onto `hrf_road`. Útgarðr's gate at (13,0) stays shut until M10.
- **Killing frost:** every outdoor Hrímfjöll screen is `cold`. In the cold, a frost bar fills over 4 s; once full, Ask loses half a heart every second until warm again (indoors, by a fire, or out of the region). The ember byrnie keeps it out entirely, so Ask can step onto the high road for a moment and learn why, but cannot cross Hrímfjöll without the byrnie. Dungeon rooms are not cold. The solver treats a cold screen as closed without the byrnie.
- **Ice sliding:** a new terrain, `glaze` (glacier ice). Stepping onto it starts a slide in that direction, which carries Ask on at 2 px a tick until a wall or the edge of the ice stops it; Ask cannot turn mid-slide. Rocks, stakes and frozen foes on the ice are what Ask stops against. Puzzles are about where a slide ends. The solver follows slides.
- **Blizzards:** in Hrímfjöll a snow day is a blizzard. A white veil closes in to a circle round Ask (wider than Niflmýrr's fog), and the wind pushes Ask gently along. The lantern does not help; Ljós does (as in the fog). Blizzards are outdoors only and never in D7.
- **The beacon-keeper Ormr** lives in a stone hut on `hrf_beacon`. His beacon's lens cracked in the first frost. Trading step 7: the dwarf-glass lens goes to Ormr, who lights the beacon and gives the beacon arm-ring (`ring_beacon`, which marks secrets on the map): "Every trade is made." Hrímfjöll's warp stone stands on the beacon hill.
- **Embla's third letter** (`q_letters` 3): given at the Refuge once Hrímfjöll is reached. It leads to a cairn at the top of the world (`hrf_cairn`), where she left the last seiðr vessel.
- **The ice mirror:** held on the item button, it stands Ask still behind a raised mirror. Ask can still turn. Any beam or rime bolt that reaches Ask's front leaves the way Ask faces, so facing the beam's source sends it straight back. Taken from Svellr's chamber in D7.
- **Light beams:** a beam (`beam`) is a shaft of rime-light from a window or crystal, traced straight across a room every tick. Prisms (`prism`, `/` or `\`) turn it 90°; a struck prism turns a quarter. A crystal eye (`eye`) lit by a beam sets its flag for good (a door opens). A Bragð beam that hits an eye lights it once too. Beams are harmless light; rime bolts from foes and the boss are what hurt.
- **Captives:** D7 holds Ása the weaver and Bjarni the fisher. Killing Hrímgerðr frees them, and `q_captives` reaches 8: everyone taken in the raid is home.
- **Thanes:** `st_thane_hrimgerdr`, and `q_thanes` reaches 4. Kolbeinn: "All four. Then there is only the King, and me. Come to Útgarðr, farmhand."
- **Hrímturn's door** at `hrf_towerfoot` is sealed until the other three thanes fall (`q_thanes` ≥ 3). That is always true when Ask arrives with the byrnie, but the seal is still checked, as the GDD asks.
- **Main quest `q_rime`** ("The Rime Tower"):
  1. Hrímfjöll reached.
  2. Hrímturn entered.
  3. The ice mirror won.
  4. Hrímgerðr dead.
  5. Final stage text: "Four thanes down. Útgarðr's gate stands open, and the Rime King is waking."

### Hrímturn (D7, 32 rooms, M9b)
- **Theme:** a tower of rime and glass the giantess grew out of the glacier. Glaze floors, beams of cold light through ice windows, prisms and crystal eyes, and frost wisps in the high halls.
- **The new mechanic, the mirror:** taught right after Svellr in a safe room (a beam onto an eye that only a raised mirror can reach), tested in three rooms (a turn, a rime bolt sent back, a beam carried across a slide), then combined with prisms before the boss.
- **The loop:**
  1. The map and compass.
  2. Slide rooms and a prism puzzle to the mini-boss.
  3. Svellr guards the mirror.
  4. Mirror and prism rooms open the way to the big key.
  5. Hrímgerðr.
- **Extras:** one heart piece behind an optional beam puzzle (`hp_d7_beam`), a secret room with an ore cache, the heart container `d7_hc`, and captive cells with Ása and Bjarni.
- **Hrímgerðr, the Glass** (a giantess of rime, boss bar "Hrímgerðr", 3 phases):
  1. She stands at the hall's far end and casts a rime bolt (tell: her hand glows). Sent back by the mirror, it strikes her and she kneels, open to the sword.
  2. At 2/3 health she casts two bolts in a row, and the floor turns to glaze, so Ask slides between the bolts.
  3. At 1/3 health icicles fall from the roof (tell: their shadows), and her bolts come faster.
  - On her death: the heart container, a warp point at the tower's foot, the captives' chains break, and Kolbeinn's line.
