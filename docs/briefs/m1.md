# M1 brief — Vertical slice

Approved by the user on 2026-09-26 (with the M1a plan). M1 must be playable from New Game to the Rótvættr fight in 45–60 minutes. The spec roadmap row is in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-26-fimbulvetr-design.md` §4.

M1 has three sub-milestones. Each ends green, playable and with a playtest:

| Sub | Content | Main systems |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **M1a Farm days** (this session) | Askdalr: 8 screens and 3 interiors, 13 NPCs, prologue days 1–3, 3 evening scenes with Embla | Sim split, interiors with a fade transition, one-way ledges, dialogue, cutscenes, quests, NPCs, lift/carry/throw/push, tall-grass cover, shop, HUD and UI scene, bitmap font, texture gallery |
| M1b Raid + road | Night-3 raid under an autumn storm, the morning legend, Myrkviðr (9 screens and 1 interior), 4 NPCs plus Kolbeinn | Enemy AI and telegraphs, hurt path, vargr, draugr (night-only) and raid trolls (scripted), leaf cover, storm weather, darkness and lantern, NPC schedules, pause menu (map and quests) |
| M1c Rótarhellir | D1: 12 rooms, Rótvættr | Dungeon doors and keys, chests, map and compass, boomerang, root-biter, sap (slow), pushable roots, vines, boss framework, heart container, solver v1, route test, v2 save fixture if the format changed |

**Myrkviðr (M1b):**
- 9 screens: `myr_road_s`, `myr_road`, `myr_clearing`, `myr_pines`, `myr_brook`, `myr_charcoal`, `myr_hollow`, `myr_roots` (D1 entrance), `myr_deep`.

**Rótarhellir (M1c):** `d1_r01`–`d1_r12`.
- Contents: entrance, vine room, sap room, pushable-root puzzle, 2 small-key rooms, map room, compass room, boomerang chest (mid-dungeon), heart-piece puzzle, secret silver cache, and the boss room.

### Story and quests (quest status is derived from flags)
**Prologue** (late summer, clock `held`). You start in the longhouse on the morning of day 1 with Halvar's **hand-axe** (new weapon id `handaxe`) and no shield.

| Day | Chores (`q_chores`) |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Herd 5 sheep into the pen (movement, pushing). Fetch water: lift the pail at the well and set it by the trough (lift and carry). |
| 2 | Split firewood. Small logs break with ordinary hits; big logs need the charged spin. |
| 3 | Scare the ravens off the barley by lifting and throwing stones and pots. |

- Chores pay silver.
- Sigrún sells the lantern and Embla's flatbread.
- Each evening Embla finds Ask (3 cutscenes, each ending on a joke left hanging). Sleeping in the bed moves to the next morning.

**The raid (M1b):**
- Night 3, the season is set to autumn with a storm. You escape the burning longhouse with the pitchfork.
- Draugr can be fought. The trolls are scripted.
- Kolbeinn takes Embla and the eight villagers, and Halvar is wounded at the gate.
- In the morning Halvar gives Ask the seax and shield. Gyða tells the legend (`q_legend`), and the clock starts cycling.

**Act I opening (M1c):**
- `q_runestone_1`: reach Rótarhellir, get the boomerang, defeat Rótvættr and light the first runestone.
- Goal flag: `st_stone1_lit`.

**Flags (M1a):**
- `st_farm_day` (int 1–3): the story day. It is separate from `clock.day`, so staying up past midnight doesn't break the chores.
- Chores: `q_sheep_d1`, `q_water_d1`, `q_logs` (int), `q_ravens` (int)
- Chore pay: `q_paid_d1`, `q_paid_d2`, `q_paid_d3`
- Evening scenes: `ev_embla_d1`, `ev_embla_d2`, `ev_embla_d3`
- `st_raid_begun` (the M1a exit beat)

Later sub-milestones add `st_raid_done`, `st_seax_given`, `st_legend_told`, `st_d1_entered`, `st_d1_boss_dead`, `st_stone1_lit`.

**Enemies in M1:**
- raven (M1a critter)
- vargr
- draugr (night-only)
- root-biter
- raid troll (scripted)
- Rótvættr (boss: stun the bulbs with the boomerang, cut the core, 3 phases)

**Heart pieces in M1:** 2 in Askdalr (the ridge rocks, and Ulf's lost-lamb errand on day 3 as an optional side task), 2 in Myrkviðr, 1 in D1. Plus 1 heart container.

## M1b and M1c in detail

Added 2026-09-27 with the M1b + M1c plan (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-27-m1b-m1c.md`). Rooms and screens are drafts until the integrity tests and the progression solver accept them.

### The raid (night 3)
Sleeping on day 3 runs a new `raid_begins`:
- The season changes to autumn and it is 01:00.
- Ask holds the **pitchfork**, the storm is on, and a card reads "You wake to fire".

**Where it happens:**
- **Longhouse:** fire hazards and one draugr, which teaches the attack tell.
- **Farmyard:** burning gates seal the W, E and S exits. There are fightable draugr, and immortal raid trolls on patrol.

**At the gate:** a trigger at `ask_gate` runs `raid_gate`:
- Kolbeinn (cutscene-only NPC) takes Embla and the eight villagers.
- Halvar is wounded at the gate.
- A card, then morning at 07:00: `st_raid_done`, and the storm ends.

**The morning:**
- Halvar, in bed, gives the seax and shield (`st_seax_given`).
- Gyða at the hof tells the legend: `st_legend_told`, the clock policy becomes `cycling`, and `q_runestone_1` starts.
- The palisade gate on `ask_gate` opens. It is a `gate` fixture, closed while `st_legend_told` is not set.

**Rules around the raid:**
- The clock is frozen from `st_raid_begun` until `st_raid_done`, so the raid night never dawns.
- The taken villagers' `places` get `not(st_raid_begun)`.
- Optional, droppable: Ulf's lost-lamb errand on day 3 with the second Askdalr heart piece (`q_lamb`).

**Enemies and cover:**
- Vargr by day and night; draugr only at night (enemy `when: phase night`).
- Autumn leaf cover (`%`) slows to 80%. The sword, the spin and the boomerang clear it. Pickups under standing leaves are hidden.

**NPCs:**
- Önundr, Dagný, Skeggi and Arnbjörg each get three or more dialogue states in en and sv, plus night variants.
- Halvar, Gyða, Sigrún and Grímr get after-raid lines.

### Rótarhellir — D1 (grid `layout.dungeons.d1`, 3×4; a draft that the solver proves in M1c)
```
        c0                c1                  c2
r0  d1_r10 secret cache  d1_r12 Rótvættr     d1_r09 heart-piece puzzle
r1  d1_r07 boomerang     d1_r11 pre-boss     d1_r08 compass
r2  d1_r05 key 2 (clear) d1_r04 root puzzle  d1_r06 map
r3  d1_r02 vines         d1_r01 entrance     d1_r03 sap, key 1
```

**Rooms:**
- `r01` is safe. Its door leads back to `myr_roots`.
- `r02` teaches vines and the root-biter.
- `r03` teaches sap and holds key 1.
- `r04` has pushable roots. It opens the shutter to `r05`, and holds lock A to `r11` (a shortcut).
- `r05` is a clear-the-room shutter room with key 2. Lock B leads to `r07`.
- `r07` has the boomerang chest (mid-dungeon).
- Boomerang switches:
  - across the sap pit, `r07`→`r11`
  - the boss door `r11`→`r12`, which combines pushing and the boomerang
- `r09` is optional: the heart piece, with boomerang and roots.
- `r10` is optional: a dark room with braziers (lantern) and the silver cache.
- The lantern is never required.

**The boss and the ending:**
- **Rótvættr:**
  1. Stunning all three bulbs with the boomerang exposes the core; the sword hits it.
  2. The bulbs recover faster, and root-biters are summoned.
  3. Root spikes erupt under Ask.
- On death: the heart container appears, the shutters open, and the runestone lights (`st_stone1_lit`).
- Kolbeinn cameo, then a warp out to `myr_roots` and "To be continued".

**Flags:**
- M1b: `st_raid_done`, `st_seax_given`, `st_legend_told` (plus optional `q_lamb_found`, `q_lamb_done`).
- M1c: `st_d1_entered`, `st_d1_boss_dead`, `st_stone1_lit`.

**Quests:** `q_legend`, `q_runestone_1`.
