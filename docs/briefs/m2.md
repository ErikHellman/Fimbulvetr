# M2 brief — Uppvík and the turning world

Approved with the M2 plan (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-27-m2.md`) on 2026-09-27. The spec roadmap row is in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-26-fimbulvetr-design.md` §4.

M2 has three parts. Each ends green, playable and with a playtest:

| Part | Content | Main systems |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **M2a Turning world** | Myrkviðr turns with the seasons and weather; Gyða's hof saves | Rolled weather (rain, wind, fog, snow), wind, snow/drift/mud/ice cover, spawn tables, forest trolls ↔ stone, rest-and-save, title screen with 3 slots, settings UI with remapping |
| **M2b Uppvík** | The road north, `myr_north`, Uppvík (4 screens, 5 interiors, 12 NPCs), the Eldr quest | Economy, gear, seiðr and mead, galdr casting, Eldr, fire spread |
| **M2c Deep Myrkviðr** | `myr_fen`, `myr_glade`, `myr_trollskog`, the völva, the huldra, the vargar hunt | vargr alpha, rime raven, seasonal herbs, overworld solver proofs |

### Story, quests and flags

| Quest | Stages |
| --- | --- |
| `q_uppvik` (M2b) | Önundr saws the pine (`st_road_open`) → reach Uppvík (`st_uppvik_reached`, a trigger at `upp_gate`) → Þórdís's welcome and horn (`w_horn_thordis`) |
| `q_eldr` (M2b) | Sölvi asks for a charred stave (`q_eldr_asked`) → Skeggi gives it (item `charred_stave`) → the lesson (`st_eldr_learned`, `inv.galdr += eldr`). Seiðr is introduced in this story beat. |
| `q_vargar` (M2c) | The Þing-stone notice (`q_vargar_taken`) → Dagný's tracking lines → kill the pack leader on `myr_north` (`q_vargar_alpha`, set by the enemy's `onDeath`) → Bersi pays: purse 300 (`q_vargar_done`) |
| `q_volva` (M2c) | Meet Heiðr: a horn, and she brews red and green mead → bring 3 fen-moss (int `q_volva_moss`; autumn only) → the blue mead recipe (`q_volva_done`) |
| `q_huldra` (M2c, night only) | Her bargain: the winter cloak for a promise (`q_huldra_promise`), to be kept or broken in a later milestone; or refuse (`q_huldra_refused`), and she returns another night |

- **New `n_*_met` flags** for every new NPC.
- **New items:**
  - `horn`: an upgrade. Meads need an empty horn.
  - `winter_cloak`: an upgrade. It halves snow slowdown.
  - `charred_stave`: a quest item.
  - `fen_moss`: an ingredient.
- **Gear prices:**
  - Uppvík sword 80 (combo 3/3/6, spin 6)
  - byrnie 100 (reduces damage taken by 25%, never below 1)
  - red mead 20, green mead 25, horn 40
- **Purse:** the starting cap of 100 fits every M2 price; the 300 purse is the hunt reward.
- **Seiðr:** starts at 10. The bar shows once a galdr is known. It is refilled by green or blue mead, seiðr-jar drops and hof prayer.

### Enemies (+3), weather and spawns
- **`forest_troll`** (M2a core, M2c content):
  - Night only, reuses the troll behaviour, immortal and guarded.
  - At sunrise it turns into a liftable `troll_stone` that breaks into silver.
- **`vargr_alpha`** (M2c): the pack leader. A howl tell summons up to 2 vargr; a heavier lunge; 14 hp.
- **`rime_raven`** (M2c): the Rime King's raven at night. It circles; if it spots Ask, a 24-tick shriek tell calls a vargr, then it dives.
- **Weather** is rolled per region and day from the GDD table. Its effects:
  - rain: Eldr halved, outdoor braziers out, Uppvík NPCs go indoors
  - wind: bends projectiles and fire spread
  - fog: visibility radius 5 tiles, which the lantern extends
  - snow: in winter
- **Cover by season:**
  - winter snow (70%, the sword clears it), drifts (50%, only Eldr melts them)
  - spring mud next to water (75%, cannot be cleared, gone on clear days)
  - winter ice on freezing water (walkable)
- **Spawn tables** (region × season × night; night doubles the count), using authored spawn points:
  - Myrkviðr by day: vargr. By night: draugr, forest trolls and, from M2c, rime ravens.
  - Uppvík and Askdalr: none.
