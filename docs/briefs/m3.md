# M3 brief — Mýrland and Sökkva Kvern

Approved with the M3 plan (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-30-m3.md`) on 2026-09-30. The spec roadmap row is in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-26-fimbulvetr-design.md` §4.

M3 has two parts. Each ends green, playable and with a playtest:

| Part | Content | Main systems |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **M3a Mýrland** | 10 screens south-west of Myrkviðr, down the forest brook; Kári, Bárðr, Þuríðr, Ljótr, Auðr | Seasonal fords (spring floods), water that never freezes, a saved boomerang latch and a drawbridge, fishing (bite, then reel), the first enemy projectile, the water-worm and the bog-light |
| **M3b Sökkva Kvern** | D2 (16 rooms) and Lindormr, then the bomb secrets back in Mýrland | Water levels set by mill wheels, bombs (ammo, blast, cracks), the big lock, the mud-crab |

| [0,8] | `myl_fisher` | Kári's hut (door) and a jetty over a spring-fed cove, so it can be fished in winter too. |

| [0,9] | `myl_mill` | The millpond, the sunken mill's roof, and Þuríðr's house (door). The mill door is barred in M3a ("the water is too high") and opens into D2 in M3b. |

### Enemies (+3, plus the boss and its mounds) and spawns
- **`vatnormr`** (water-worm; en "Water-worm", sv "Vattenorm"; M3a):
  - `swims`: it is placed on water and never leaves it.
  - It lies submerged and untouchable, surfaces with a 24-tick tell, then spits the game's **first enemy projectile**, which the shield blocks.
  - It stays up for 40 ticks, open to a blow; the boomerang stuns it. 3 hp.
- **`myrljos`** (en "Bog-light", sv "Lyktgubbe"; M3a):
  - Night only; it `flies` and has a `glow`.
  - It drifts and fades out (untouchable for 60 ticks), then flares 3 tiles from Ask (24-tick tell) and darts. 2 hp; drops seiðr.
- **`leirkrabbi`** (en "Mud-crab", sv "Gyttjekrabba"; M3b):
  - Its shell guards against everything, but `cracks: 'force'` means a bomb breaks it, and then the sword finishes it.
  - Sidles and pinches (24-tick tell). 12 hp; drops bombs; `needs: ['bombs']`.
- **Spawn table `myrland`** (2 per season, ×2 at night; the crab joins in M3b):

| Season | Day | Night |

### D2 — Sökkva Kvern (4×4; `myl_mill` → `d2_r01`)
```
        c0             c1               c2               c3
r0   r13 piece      r14 cache        r15 Lindormr     r16 stone
r1   r09 floats-2   r10 great wheel  r11 big key      r12 tail-race
r2   r05 switches   r06 millstone    r07 bombs        r08 crabs
r3   r03 cellar     r01 wheel-house  r02 mill-race    r04 sump
```
- **r01, the wheel-house (safe room).**
  - Wheels for levels 0 and 1 stand on a landing that no level changes.
  - West, a sluice floor that is dry at level 0 leads to r03. East, race planks that float at level ≥1 lead to r02. North is r06.
  - This one room teaches both effects of the water.
- **r03, the cellar.** Drained channels at level 0; two draugr; the map chest.
- **r05, the grain loft.** Two boomerang switches across water open a shutter to key 1.
- **r02, the mill-race.** A crossing on floats at level 1, with two water-worms spitting (the shield lesson); key 2.
- **r04, the sump (a combination room).** Lower the water with its own wheel to reach the compass, then boomerang its level-1 wheel from the landing to float back.
- **r06, the millstone hall (the hub).** Lock A east, lock B north.
- **r07, the powder store.** A `clear` trap reveals the **bombs** chest (10). Bomb pots; a first crack; a ledge shortcut down into r02.
- **r08, the crab pen.** Two mud-crabs guard key 3; an optional crack south to r04.
- **r12, the tail-race.** A water-worm and a crab; lock C west to r11.
- **r10, the great wheel.** The level-2 wheel hangs across a pit, so only the boomerang reaches it. A crack north to r14.
- **r09.** Its planks float at level 2 and lead to r13.
- **r13, the piece.** Drain to 0 → bomb the crack → boomerang a level-2 wheel → the planks rise → `hp_d2_r13`.
- **r14.** The secret silver cache, and bomb pots.
- **r11.** The big key (appears on `clear`) and the new **big lock** north to the boss.
- **r15, Lindormr's pond.** After the fight, `d2_hc` appears and a shutter opens east.
- **r16.** The runestone (`stone2_light`), a Kolbeinn cameo, and a door out to `myl_mill`, as D1 ends.

**Lindormr** (24 hp; phases at 16 and 8; `needs: ['bombs']`). Four `lind_mound`s are its summons: each is guarded, has `cracks: 'force'` and always drops bombs.
1. **Phase 1.** It hides in one mound, which bubbles as the tell. Every 120 ticks it rears (a 24-tick tell) and spits. Bombing its mound flushes it out, stunned for 150 ticks and open to the sword. Then it burrows into another mound; a broken mound regrows after 240 ticks.
2. **Phase 2.** It moves underground after every spit (a 40-tick ripple), a decoy mound bubbles too, and it spits twice.
3. **Phase 3 (a new pattern, not more hp).** Once flushed out it may coil (a 30-tick tell) and charge in a line. It is dazed for 90 ticks when it hits a wall.
