# M7 brief — Sævatn, the Refuge and Sökkva Hof

Approved by the user on 2026-10-04 with the recommended answers: M6 is built first, Sökkva Hof's door opens in any season, and the Norns' loom stays in M7. It is built after M6. The spec roadmap row is in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-26-fimbulvetr-design.md` §4: *M7 Sævatn + Refuge + D5: swim/dive, Vindr, Refuge hub, Embla joins, Norns' loom; 13 screens, D5 24 rooms + Nykr; 12–14 sessions*. Choices the GDD leaves open are listed under "Decisions" as Claude's calls. The questions for the user are at the end, each with a recommended answer.

**This brief assumes M6 as drafted in `docs/briefs/m6.md`, with its recommended answers:** Eldr melts the rime wall, the seal-skin quest comes in M7, and M5b goes up as its own PR. M6 is not approved or built yet, and M7 cannot be built before it. "Depends on M6" below lists exactly what M7 takes from it.

M7 is the middle of Act II. Ask earns the seal-skin from the seal-hunter on the Niflmýrr shore, swims out across Sævatn to the island Holmr, and finds Embla at the Refuge. From there Ask dives into the drowned temple Sökkva Hof, takes the Vindr galdr and kills the second thane, Nykr the Tide. The Norns' loom under the lake lets Ask turn the season at any hof. M7 has two parts, and each ends green, playable and with a playtest:

### What the code check turned up (it shapes the design)
- **Ids already there:**
  - Region `saevatn` and dungeon `d5`.
  - Sub-item `sealskin` ("Seal-skin" / "Sälskinn") and the sound `sfx_seal`.
  - Galdr `vindr` (3 seiðr).
  - NPC `embla`, her schedule `embla_evening`, and `flatbread` ("Embla's flatbread").
- **The ferry:** Bárðr's landing `myl_ferry` sits at grid (1,8), in a channel the warm springs keep open all winter. `st_pass_open` already means "the ferry runs", but nothing rows anywhere yet.
- **Water:** water is solid but open above (things in flight cross it). Screens can already hold a water level in a flag (`world/water.ts`, the D2 mill wheels), and ground cover can lay walkable ice over water. Both are reused for the frozen lake and the flooded rooms.
- **Seasons:** the clock has a season and a policy (`cycling` or held), and conditions can test the season. Nothing lets the player choose one yet.
- **Save:** `SAVE_VERSION` stays 1. Every M7 id is appended (flags, items, quests, pieces, chests). Swimming, diving and currents live on the hero entity and the tile under it, never in `GameState`. `GameState`, `SaveData`, `Sim` and `ContentDb` keep their shape. Turning the season needs one new effect kind (append-only); the plan spells it out before it is written.

### Decisions (Claude's calls, for the user to overrule)
- **Where Sævatn sits:** 13 screens in grid columns 0–2, rows 1–7, between Mýrland (below), Niflmýrr (east of the north end) and the cliffs of Myrkviðr and Uppvík (east), which close that side. There are two ways in: Bárðr's ferry or the winter ice from `myl_ferry` in the south, and the shore walk from `nif_shore` in the north-east.
- **Swimming with the seal-skin:**
  - Owning the skin is enough; it is never readied in a slot. Deep water becomes swimmable.
  - Ask swims at 70% speed and cannot use the sword, sub-items or galdr while swimming, so water is where you are weakest.
  - The roll button dives for 2 s: Ask goes under foes' attacks, floating logs and nets, and picks things off the bottom at dive spots (a ripple marks them).
  - Currents push a swimmer one way. Strong currents can only be crossed by diving under them.
  - Ís on water while Ask swims: the ice forms round Ask, who climbs out onto it.
- **The lake in winter:** after the Fimbulvetr the lake freezes and Ask can walk out across it from the ferry landing. Holmr is ringed by a warm current that never freezes, though, so the Refuge needs the seal-skin in every season (spec §1 fix 12). The ferry rows in the other three seasons, and only as far as the near landing.
- **Embla joins the Refuge, not the party** (spec §1 fix 15: story-only in Act II). She is there when Ask first reaches Holmr; `st_embla_found` is set on that first meeting. She runs the war table and gives her letters. She does not fight.
- **Captives:** D5 holds Oddr and Hallbera. Killing Nykr frees them and `q_captives` reaches 4. Each brings a service home to Askdalr: Oddr the boat-builder lays a skiff at `myl_ferry`, which rows from there to the near landing even before Ask meets Bárðr again; Hallbera brews mead again and refills red mead at her door for 20 silver. Bjarni, Ása, Þorkell and Rannveig wait for D6, D7 and the Refuge.
- **Thanes:** `st_thane_nykr`, and `q_thanes` reaches 2. Kolbeinn appears again after Nykr falls: "Half his oath is water now. You will drown in the rest."
- **The Refuge:**
  - The healer is a new NPC, **Vala**, who heals Ask for free once a day and sells green mead.
  - The ore-trader **Hreggviðr** takes ore, not silver. Ore (`ore`, a new counted item) comes from D5's chests and from black rocks broken with bombs on Sævatn. He sells blue mead, a quiver upgrade, and the arm-ring of the beacon in M8 (named but not given yet).
  - The war table shows `q_captives` of 8 and `q_thanes` of 4, with each freed name and each dead thane.
  - Ore also pays for farm stages 3–5 later (GDD), so M7 adds the item and the trader, not the farm stages.
- **Embla's letters:** the first letter (`q_letters` 1) comes in M7 and leads to a place from her childhood in Myrkviðr, where a carved box holds a seiðr vessel. Letters 2 and 3 come in M8 and M9, and the third frames the ending, as the GDD says.
- **Trading chain step 5:** the walrus-ivory comb goes to Embla, who gives her sail-needle (`trade_needle`): "For a dwarf who mends bellows and swears at them." That points at Dvergagröf (step 6, M8).
- **Sökkva Hof's door:** the drowned hof's spire stands in `sae_drowned`. In spring, summer and autumn Ask dives at its ripple. In winter it is under the ice, and Eldr melts a hole over it (like the burbot's hole). See question 2.
- **The Norns' loom is optional,** as in the GDD. It needs swimming, so it is open from M7a, but its threads are spread so that it naturally finishes after D5 (one thread needs Vindr).
- **Turning the season:** after the loom, praying at a hof (Gyða's hof in Askdalr and the shrine at the Refuge) offers the four seasons. The choice sets the season and restarts its 6-day count. Dungeons keep their clocks paused, and Hrímfjöll stays winter until the ending.

### The way out (M7a)
- **The seal-skin (`q_sealskin`):**
  - Hrafn the seal-hunter (M6, `nif_shore`) has lost his nets every night to a marbendill, a merman who climbs the strand after dark.
  - Ask fights it off the nets three nights in a row (a small fight that does not need swimming).
  - Hrafn gives his late wife's seal-skin: "She went back to the water once. Mind you come back from it."
- **Bárðr's ferry:** once the pass is open, Bárðr rows Ask from `myl_ferry` to `sae_landing` for 10 silver, in spring, summer and autumn. In winter he points north at the ice.
- **Main quest `q_holmr`** ("The island"):
  1. The seal-skin won.
  2. Holmr reached, and Embla found.
  3. Sökkva Hof entered.
  4. Vindr learned.
  5. Nykr dead.
  6. Final stage text: "Two thanes down. Embla says the third keeps a forge under the mountain."

### Sökkva Hof (D5, 24 rooms, M7b)
- **Theme:** the drowned temple of the old lake-folk. Half the rooms are flooded, and their water stands at one of three levels, set by sluices.
- **The new mechanic, Vindr** (3 seiðr):
  - A gust the way Ask faces, 5 tiles. It pushes foes (and the boss) back two tiles, scatters leaves and weed to show what lies under them, spins wind fans that work sluices, and fills a sail to carry a raft across a still pool.
  - Taught by a carved stave behind Hrönn, tested in three rooms, and combined with the grapple (M6) and the water levels before the boss.
- **Also:**
  - Dungeon currents that carry a swimmer through rooms on fixed lanes.
  - Dive passages between flooded rooms.
  - The water level is kept per dungeon in a flag, as in D2.
- **The loop:**
  1. The map and compass.
  2. The first sluice, which drains the upper hall.
  3. Hrönn guards Vindr.
  4. The fans and sails, which reach the big key.
  5. Nykr.
- **Extras:**
  - One heart piece behind an optional current puzzle: `hp_d5_r17`.
  - A secret room with an ore cache.
  - The heart container `d5_hc`.
  - Captive cells with Oddr and Hallbera.
- **Nykr, the Tide** (a water horse, boss bar "Nykr", 3 phases):
  1. Nykr circles a pool round a stone island and surges waves across it. A Vindr gust while it rears pushes it onto the stone, where it flounders and can be struck.
  2. At 2/3 health it floods the room. Ask swims and dives under its charge, and a fan on the wall (Vindr) drains the pool again for one strike.
  3. At 1/3 health it rides a whirlpool in the room's middle. The grapple (M6) hooks its bridle and pulls it out onto the stone.
  - On its death: the heart container, a warp point at the spire, the captives' chains break, and Kolbeinn's line.

### The Norns' loom (M7b, optional)
- **The way in:** a dive at the whirlpool in `sae_well` leads to `sae_int_well`, Urðr's well, where the three Norns sit at a loom with three empty warps.
- **Quest `q_loom`:** three threads (`norn_thread`, max 3), one from each lowland region beyond home:
  - Myrkviðr: caught in a web that only Vindr can blow clear.
  - Mýrland: on the bottom of the warm-spring channel at `myl_ferry`, by a dive.
  - Haugar: on a barrow at night, shown by Ljós (M6).
- **Reward:** a seiðr vessel (+5), `st_loom_woven`, and the hofs' new power to turn the season.

### Depends on M6
M7 builds straight on M6. These are the pieces it takes, so M6 must land first:
1. **Niflmýrr's shore and Hrafn.** The seal-skin giver lives at `nif_shore` (3,1), and Sævatn's north end (`sae_fjordmouth`) opens onto it. The grid layout above assumes M6's Niflmýrr in columns 3–7, rows 0–1.
2. **The trading chain's step 4.** Embla's step 5 takes M6's `trade_comb`.
3. **The thane and captive framework.** `q_thanes`, `q_captives`, the cells and the captives' homecoming come from M6 and Náströnd. M7 only adds Nykr, Oddr and Hallbera.
4. **The twist.** Embla's first scene answers what the dying thrall at `nif_camp` says.
5. **The grapple chain.** D5's combo rooms and Nykr's third phase use it.
6. **Ís on water.** Swimming has to handle the ice Ís lays, so Ís comes first.
7. **Ljós.** The loom's Haugar thread is shown by it.
8. **`fog_draugr` and the rune-staves:** not needed, but D5 reuses the stave pedestal room from D4.
9. **What does not depend on M6:** swimming and diving, currents, Vindr, flooded rooms and the season turn are systems of their own. If you wanted, they could be built ahead, but they still could not be played until M6's shore exists.
