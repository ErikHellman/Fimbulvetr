### Warp stones (M4a places them; M4b adds Farvegr)
- A new thing `{k:'warp', region, at, arrive}`: a rune-cut standing stone. Interact wakes it for good (`world.warps`, which the save has had since M0).
- One per lowland region: Askdalr (`ask_village`), Myrkviðr (`upp_square`), Mýrland (`myl_river`), Haugar (`hau_circle`).

### Techniques (flags, bought from Styrr)
- **Dash thrust (`t_dash`):** the sword pressed during a roll turns it into a lunge. It deals double the first swing, reaches further, and **pierces a shield**: a barrow-wight's guard does not stop it.
- **Parry (`t_parry`):** a melee blow that lands within the first 10 ticks of raising the shield is parried, even a heavy one. It deals no damage and stuns the attacker for 60 ticks.

### Runestone scaling
- Foes in the lowland overworld (Askdalr, Myrkviðr, Mýrland, Haugar) grow with the stones lit, bosses and dungeon rooms excepted:
  - two stones: +50% health;
  - three stones: +100% health and +1 quarter heart on every blow.
- None of it applies before the second stone, so every older route, fixture and hash is unchanged.

### Enemies (+3, plus a mini-boss and a boss) and spawns
- **`haugbui`** (en "Barrow-wight", sv "Högbo"; M4a):
  - An armoured draugr with a round shield. Blows from the front clink off, unless they are a dash thrust or land while it swings.
  - It circles, raises its blade (a 24-tick tell) and cuts. The shield blocks the cut, and the parry turns it.
  - 8 hp; weak to fire; drops silver.
- **`bogdraugr`** (en "Draugr archer", sv "Bågdrög"; M4b):
  - It keeps its distance, draws (a 24-tick tell) and looses an arrow, which the shield blocks.
  - 4 hp; weak to fire; drops arrows once the bow is owned.
- **`haugvordr`** (en "Barrow-warden", sv "Högväktare"; M4b mini-boss, on the boss bar):
  - A giant shielded draugr, 16 hp. Its shield-bash charge (a 30-tick tell) leaves it dazed for 90 ticks against a wall, open from every side. A sweeping cut (24-tick tell) when Ask is close.
  - Once dead it stays dead (`st_d3_warden`), and the bow's chest appears.

### D3 — Konungshaugr (5×4; `hau_king` → `d3_r01`)
```
        c0             c1              c2             c3             c4
r0   r16 hounds     r17 gallery     r19 the King   r20 stone      r18 cache
r1   r11 dark tomb  r12 archers     r13 champions  r14 bridge     r15 piece
r2   r06 ossuary    r07 west hall   r08 hub        r09 warden     r10 eye hall
r3   r03 map        r02 gold        r01 entrance   r04 ghosts     r05 compass
```
- **r01, the entrance (safe room).** Lit braziers and a rune sign: "Take nothing." Exits west, east and north.
- **r02, the gold lesson.**
  - A hoard of grave-gold on a dais, with two draugr asleep beside it.
  - The shutter north stays shut until the room is clear, and sleepers count. Lifting the gold wakes them.
- **r03, the map room.** Dark, with one lit brazier and two cold ones; two draugr; the map.
- **r04, the ghost lesson.** A lit room with pits and a ghost floor that shows near the braziers. Key 1 lies on the island.
- **r05, the compass.** An eye switch across the pits lowers a bridge to the compass (bow).
- **r06, the ossuary.** Dark: the ghost path is lit only by the lantern or braziers along it. Key 2.
- **r07, the west hall.** A barrow-wight and pots. The far side of r02's shutter.
- **r08, the hub.** Lock A east, lock B north.
- **r09, the warden.** Behind lock A: trap shutters and Haugvörðr. On clear, the **bow**'s chest.
- **r10, the eye hall.** Two draugr archers on ledges across the pits. An eye switch latches a bridge to key 3.
- **r13, the champions.** Behind lock B: two barrow-wights (the thrust pays off here). West to r12, east to r14, north the big lock.
- **r12, the archers.**
  - Archers across the pits. An eye switch lowers the bridge to lock C, and lock C opens west to r11.
  - So lock C is always behind the bow, and no key order strands Ask: three keys, three locks.
- **r11, the dark tomb.** Three sleepers, grave-gold on a ghost-floor island, in the dark. The **big key** appears on clear.
- **r16, r17.** The hounds' crypt and the shield gallery: an optional loop from r11 to r12, with arrow pots and a silver chest.
- **r14, the bridge.** An eye switch latches a bridge east to r15.
- **r15, the piece (a combination room).**
  - Dark, with a ghost floor, grave-gold piled on the path, sleepers and an eye switch.
  - `hp_d3_r15` lies at the end. A crack in the north wall leads to r18.
- **r18.** The secret silver cache, and arrow pots.
- **r19, the King's hall.** Behind the big lock. Arrow pots in the corners. After the fight `d3_hc` appears and a shutter opens east.
- **r20, the stone.** `stone3_light`: Ask learns **Farvegr**. A Kolbeinn cameo, then out to `hau_king`.

**The Haugbúi King** (24 hp; phases at 16 and 8; `needs: ['bow']`). Guarded at all times except when stunned.
1. **Phase 1.**
   - It stalks and sweeps its sword when Ask is close (a 24-tick tell).
   - Every 150 ticks it lowers its head and its crown's gems blaze (a 30-tick tell), then it charges in a line.
   - An arrow in the crown during the tell or the charge shatters a gem: it falls stunned for 150 ticks, open to the sword. A charge into a wall without that only dazes it for 40 ticks, still guarded.
2. **Phase 2.** Each time it rises, two draugr climb out of the floor, and it charges twice in a row (the second after a 20-tick tell).
3. **Phase 3 (a new pattern, not more hp).** Between charges it hurls its spectral axe, which flies out and back; the shield blocks it.

### Farvegr and readying a galdr (M4b)
- **Farvegr:**
  - Sung outdoors in the overworld (4 seiðr), it opens a picker of the woken warp stones.
  - Choosing one pays the seiðr, fades out and puts Ask by the stone. Cancelling costs nothing.
  - Underground and indoors it fizzles.
- **Readying a galdr:** the pause menu's items tab lists the galdr known, and confirm readies one for the galdr button.
