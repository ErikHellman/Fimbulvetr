### What the code check turned up (it shapes the design)
- **NPCs: 55** (the target is 45). Nothing to add.
- **Quests: 35.** Twelve are the main line (`q_chores`, `q_legend`, the three runestones, `q_uppvik`, `q_fimbulvetr`, `q_act2`, `q_holmr`, `q_forge`, `q_rime`, `q_king`). That leaves **23 side quests**, so two are added.
- **Heart pieces: 30** (29 in the world or handed over, plus `hp_nif_gjoll` from a verse). The target is 36 (nine hearts, so 3 + 8 containers + 9 = 20 hearts, the GDD's maximum). Six are added.
- **Bragi's verses** (`content/verses.ts`, M6a) already say "more are sung in later acts". They mark hidden pieces on the map, which fits the new world pieces.
- **Accessibility:** remapping, the colour-blind aid, shake, flashes, hold-to-toggle shield and long days are all in the settings menu already. **Text size** is in `Settings` (`textSize: 1 | 2 | 3`) but was kept off the menu "until the font has drawn sizes" (M2a ruling). That is the one gap.
- **No achievement code exists.** `Cond` can express almost every feat, except counts of heart pieces and warp stones.
- **Safari:** the PWA, `navigator.storage.persist()` and the export nudge are in already (`shell/platform/pwa.ts`, `saveService.ts`). WebKit is not installed in this container, so the pass is an audit plus Chromium-measured performance; the user's playtest covers Safari itself.
- **Save:** `SAVE_VERSION` stays 1, and every M11 id is appended. Achievements are kept in the browser beside the settings, not in a save. `GameState`, `SaveData`, `Sim` and `ContentDb` keep their shape.

### Decisions (Claude's calls, taken)

**Two new side quests.**
1. **The rune-record** (`q_record`, Gyða, from the moment Halvar confesses): the names of the huscarls who fell at the first binding are cut on four bauta-stones across the land, in Myrkviðr, Haugar, Sævatn and Hrímfjöll. Ask reads each one (a prop with a line). Gyða cuts the names into the hof's record and gives a heart piece.
2. **The spring feast** (`q_feast`, Halvar, post-game): Askdalr holds a feast for the homecoming. Ask brings the mead-honey from Sigrún, a burbot from Kári and a cask from the Refuge, then sits at the feast (a short script with the villagers). Halvar gives a heart piece.

**Six new heart pieces.**
- Two from the quests above (`hp_record`, `hp_feast`).
- Four hidden in the world behind late tools, each with a new verse from Bragi (30 silver, as before): one behind the grapple, one behind the hammer, one behind Ís, and one on the thawed mountain after the ending. Each goes into the solver's reachability tests.

**Achievements (24).** Each is a condition over a save. The game checks them a few times a second, and a new one shows a short toast ("Achievement: Three fires"). They are kept for the browser, across all save slots, under `fimbulvetr.achievements.v1`. The pause menu's Quests tab gains a progress footer (heart pieces x/36, side quests x/25, achievements x/24), and the title menu gains an Achievements page that lists every one (names of locked ones shown greyed, with their hint).

| Theme | Achievements |
| --- | --- |
| Story | survive the raid; light the first runestone; light all three; kill the first thane; kill all four; find Embla; end the winter |
| Choices | spare Kolbeinn; slay Kolbeinn; stay in Askdalr; sail with Embla |
| People | bring every captive home; read all of Embla's letters; finish the long trade; rebuild the whole farm; weave the Norns' thread |
| World | catch Gamli; learn every galdr; light every warp stone; find every heart piece; finish every side quest; hold 500 silver; read the rune-record; sit at the spring feast |

**Text size, three drawn sizes.** The art layer draws the font at 1×, 1.5× and 2× (each glyph scaled pixel by pixel, so the large sizes stay crisp). The setting becomes Normal, Large and Larger, and applies to dialogue, its speaker tab and choices, and story cards. The dialogue box grows upward to fit the text. Menus and the HUD stay at 1×. Since the old number was never on the menu, the setting is read fresh as a word (`'normal' | 'large' | 'larger'`, default Normal), and a stored number falls back to Normal.

**The title-screen rune.** A finished save shows a small ᛟ (othala, "home") rune before its slot line on the title screen and in the slot picker. The font gains the glyph.

**The Safari and performance pass.**
- **Audit** the shell for WebKit gaps: audio unlock on the first gesture, `localStorage` throwing in private windows, `requestIdleCallback`, `structuredClone`, `ImageData` and canvas limits, Fullscreen API prefixes, and IndexedDB eviction. Fix what is found.
- **Performance:** a sim benchmark test (a fixed number of ticks on the busiest screens must stay under a time budget), and a check that no view allocates game objects per frame in its hot path. Fix the worst offenders found by a Chromium profile of the busiest rooms.
- **Ship:** version 1.0.0, the README's "how to play", and the final credits checked. **No GitHub Pages deploy without the user's word.**

**Sailing away** (`st_end_go`) keeps M10's after-scene. A playable voyage is not added.
