### What the code check turned up (it shapes the design)
- **Ids already there:** galdr `hlif` (3 seiðr) and `bragd` (4) in `src/content/galdr.ts`; flag `st_pass_open`; rings `ring_stamina`, `ring_thrift`, `ring_beacon`, `ring_berserker`; items `seidr_upgrade`, `quiver`, `horn`, `purse`.
- **The pass:** `hau_pass` has the slab gate (closed until `st_pass_open`) and three seals that burn with the stones. Rows 0–3 above the door are solid rock, and the screen has no neighbour north.
- **The ending so far:** `stone3_light` (`src/content/scripts/haugar.ts`) ends on a "To be continued." card. M5 replaces that card with a pointer to the pass.
- **Bárðr** rows once `st_pass_open` is set (`src/content/dialogue/bardr.ts`). Sævatn is M7, so his line must change, or the demo points at a ferry to nowhere.
- **Not wired yet:** `inv.ring` is in the save but no ring has an effect, and nothing records which rings are owned; `seidr_upgrade` exists as an id but nothing grants it.
- **Save:** `SAVE_VERSION` stays 1. Every M5 id is appended (flags, items, quests, pieces), and the ward lives on the hero entity, not in `GameState`. The owned rings go in flags, so `GameState`, `SaveData` and `Sim` keep their shape.

### Decisions (Claude's calls, for the user to overrule)
- **"10 side quests" means 10 new ones.** With the six Act I side quests that exist (`q_eldr`, `q_volva`, `q_huldra`, `q_vargar`, `q_fisher`, `q_huscarl`), the trading chain and the farm, the demo's quest log holds 18. The cut line below drops four of them to M11 if the sessions overrun.
- **The duel comes before the pass.** Styrr challenges Ask once both techniques are known, so Bragð can be had before the finale, as an optional reward.
- **The demo ends in free play.** After the credits the game goes on in the Fimbulvetr winter: every side quest, both farm stages and the trading chain stay open. The gorge beyond the door is choked by a wall of rime that M6 opens.
- **No new NPCs** (41 stay 41). Existing people carry the side quests, and Hildr moves to Askdalr with farm stage 2.
- **Pass first, side content second.** M5a lands the finale so the demo has an ending early; M5b fills the lowlands around it.

### The pass and the winter snap (M5a)
- **The pointer:** `stone3_light` ends with Kolbeinn's "We will be waiting on the other side" and a fade to `hau_king`, with no card. Hallsteinn, Styrr and Gyða then point at the pass.
- **Opening (`pass_open` script):** with `st_stone3_lit`, interact at the door.
  1. The three seals flare in turn, and the door grinds open.
  2. The camera holds on the gorge: the mountain glows from within.
  3. A white wave of the Rime King's breath rolls south over the screen (a tint sweep plus snow particles).
  4. `setSeason('winter', 'story')`, then `st_pass_open`.
  5. A card, "The Fimbulvetr", then the credits.
- **The gorge:** rows 0–3 of `hau_pass` become a short gorge that ends in a rime wall (a new `rime` gate, closed until an M6 flag). Hallsteinn: "The door was the easy part."
- **Credits stub:** a Credits scene (in the shell, reusing the UI font) rolls the names and tools, then "Thank you for playing the Fimbulvetr demo. The road north continues in the full game." Any key skips it. It ends back at `hau_pass` in winter, with an autosave.
- **After the snap:** the clock cycles from winter as the GDD says. Every lowland screen must stay finishable in winter, which the solver already proves per season.
- **Bárðr:** after the snap, "The lake froze in one night. I will not row on that, and I will not walk it yet." The crossing waits for M7.

### Askdalr aftermath (M5a)
- **Ruins from the raid, kept until rebuilt:** the longhouse's scorched roof, the burned byre and sheepfold, and the eight captives' houses shut with dark windows. These are `when`-keyed things and decor, so rebuilding only flips flags.
- **Coming home in the Fimbulvetr (`q_fimbulvetr`, main):**
  1. `st_stone3_lit`: go to the pass.
  2. `st_pass_open`: the winter snap.
  3. `st_home_winter`: back in Askdalr, Halvar (still abed) knows this cold and will not say how; Gyða says the binding was sworn on blood, and the lost leaves of her rune-record would say more (starts `q_pages`).
  4. Final stage text: "The pass is open, but the road north is buried in rime." This is the demo's last main-quest line.
- **New lines (after the snap):** Halvar, Gyða, Sigrún and Grímr in Askdalr; Þórdís, Sölvi, Steinn, Ragna and Hrafnkell in Uppvík; Heiðr; Kári and Bárðr; Styrr, Hildr, Geirmundr and Hallsteinn. Each gets one Fimbulvetr line in en and sv. Everyone else keeps their current winter lines.

### Styrr's last duel and Bragð (M5a)
- **`q_huscarl`** continues: `t_parry` → `q_duel_asked` (Styrr's challenge) → `q_duel_won` → `st_bragd_learned` (`inv.galdr += bragd`).
- **The duel** is in Styrr's yard (`hau_huscarl`), by day or night, on the boss bar as "Styrr":
  - Nobody dies. At 0 hp Styrr yields; if Ask drops to one heart the duel stops ("Again?"), and hearts are restored. Leaving the yard forfeits it.
  - Phase 1: he guards with his shield and cuts (a 24-tick tell). Only the dash thrust gets through the shield.
  - Phase 2 (at half hp): a heavy overhead cut (a 30-tick tell) that staggers through the shield. Parrying it stuns him for 60 ticks, the opening.
  - 12 hp. The lesson after a win is free.
- **Bragð** (galdr button, 4 seiðr): Ask raises the sword (a 16-tick pose), then a beam flies straight for up to 14 tiles. It deals the spin's damage, pierces a shield (`PIERCE`), and stops at walls. It does not break guards that turn every blow, such as the Haugbúi King's mail.

### Hlíf (M5a)
- Sölvi teaches it once `st_pass_open` is set, for 120 silver (`st_hlif_learned`).
- Sung (3 seiðr), a ring of runes circles Ask and absorbs the next 3 hits of any kind, heavy ones included, for up to 20 s. Each absorbed hit flashes a rune. Singing it again refreshes it to 3.
- The ward lives on the hero entity: it survives screen changes, not a reload.
