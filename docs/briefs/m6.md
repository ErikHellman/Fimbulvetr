### Decisions (Claude's calls, for the user to overrule)
- **Where Niflmýrr sits:** grid columns 3–7, rows 0–1, so it is 10 screens. The gorge's north edge leads into its south-east corner, and its west end meets the Sævatn shore (M7). Cliffs close its south edge against Uppvík and Haugar, so the gorge is the only way in.
- **Fog all the time:** Niflmýrr's weather is always fog, whatever the season. You can see 5 tiles; the lantern widens that, and Ljós burns the fog off for a while. Night there is the darkest in the game. Rain never falls there, and snow falls in winter on top of the fog.
- **The twist comes in Niflmýrr, not in a cutscene:** at a drained camp on the Gjöll's bank, a dying draugr-thrall gives up the truth: the binding was sworn on the villagers' blood, and their descendants are being drained to unmake it. Embla was not among them, because she got away. That points at the Refuge (M7).
- **Captives:** Helgrind holds two of the eight, Ulf and Tófa, in cells off the Gjöll. Killing Náströnd breaks their chains. They walk home, and each brings a service back to Askdalr: Ulf herds the pasture's flock again, and his herding round can be replayed for silver; Tófa, who no longer doubts trolls, sells flatbread and cheese at her stall in the field. The other six come in D5–D7 and the Refuge. The main quest's war count (`q_captives`, int 8) starts here.
- **Thanes:** main quest `q_thanes` (int 4). Each thane's death sets its own flag (`st_thane_nastrond`). Kolbeinn appears after Náströnd falls ("Three more, and you will have done my work for me.").
- **Ljós is optional for D4.** The lantern is enough to finish Helgrind. Ljós reveals hidden paths (one heart piece) and invisible mara, and it clears fog. Heiðr teaches it for three will-o'-wisp embers caught in Niflmýrr's jars at night.
- **Rune-staves:** one-use items, one per galdr (`stave_is` now, more later). Cast from the galdr button while readied in the menu, they use no seiðr. Sölvi sells `stave_is` (40 silver) once the pass is open, which is how you sample Ís before Helgrind. In Helgrind, a carved stave in the chest past Garmr teaches Ís for good.
- **Ís:** a bolt that flies the way Ask faces (like Eldr). A foe it hits freezes for 4 s, open to a shattering blow (double damage); bosses are immune. Water it touches ices over in a 3×3 patch, in any season. The Gjöll never freezes, because it runs too fast.

### People (M6a)
- **The wandering skald (Bragi):** new NPC. By night at the drained camp's fire. He sells verses, 3 now (more later), at 30 silver each. Each verse marks one secret on the pause map. The marker is a new map layer (`world.vars`, appended).
- **The seal-hunter (Hrafn):** new NPC at `nif_shore`.
  - Trading step 4: Gamli's bone hook in exchange for a walrus-ivory comb (`trade_comb`). The comb is "for someone who still combs her hair for a reason", which points at Embla (step 5, M7).
  - He mentions the seal-skin, but his quest waits for M7 (question 2).
- **Heiðr (Ljós, `q_ljos`):** "The marsh's lights are the dead who could not cross. Bring me three of them in a jar, at night." There are three wisp embers (`wisp_ember`, max 3) in `nif_jars`, `nif_causeway` and `nif_strand`, caught at night with a lift. The reward is Ljós.
- **New lines for after the rime melts:** Gyða, Sölvi (sells the Ís stave), Styrr, Hallsteinn and Kári. One line each.

### Helgrind (D4, 22 rooms, M6b)
- **Theme:** Hel's gate over the river Gjöll. The rooms are rafts and bone-bridges over the river, rooms thick with fog (the lantern's circle is all you see), and cells.
- **The new mechanic, the grapple chain:**
  - It fires the way Ask faces, 6 tiles. It hooks a grapple post and pulls Ask across pits and water.
  - It pulls light foes and pickups to Ask, and it yanks shields away.
  - It is taught in a safe room, tested in three, then combined with the boomerang and bombs before the boss.
- **Also:**
  - Moving platforms (rafts) ride the Gjöll on fixed paths.
  - Fog rooms are dark rooms where only the lantern's circle shows.
  - One room with a rune-stave pedestal.
- **The loop:**
  1. The map and compass.
  2. Garmr guards the grapple.
  3. The Ís stave chest past Garmr.
  4. The big key across the river.
  5. Náströnd.
- **Extras:**
  - One heart piece behind an optional grapple puzzle: `hp_d4_r14`.
  - A secret room with a silver cache (100).
  - The heart container `d4_hc`.
  - Captive cells with Ulf and Tófa.
- **Náströnd, the Hollow** (draugr lord, boss bar "Náströnd", 3 phases):
  1. A tower shield turns every blow. Grapple the shield away, and he stands open while he walks back to fetch it. Sword.
  2. At 2/3 health he raises two `fog_draugr`, and his shield's return is quicker.
  3. At 1/3 health the room fills with fog (the lantern's circle only), and he strikes from it. The new pattern is a sweeping chain-flail that the shield blocks.
  - On his death: the heart container, a warp point at the gate, the captives' chains break, and Kolbeinn's line.

### Heart pieces and progress
- **Pieces:** +4 (`hp_nif_deadwood`, `hp_nif_gjoll`, `hp_nif_cairns`, `hp_d4_r14`), for 22 of 36. Hearts: one container.
- **Seiðr:** no upgrade in M6. The next one is in M7.
