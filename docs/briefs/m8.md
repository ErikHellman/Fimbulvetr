### What the code check turned up (it shapes the design)
- **Ids already there:** region `dvergagrof` and dungeon `d6`; sub-item `hammer`; galdr `skjalfti` (5 seiðr); weapon `dwarf_blade` and armour `ember_byrnie` (damage reduction 0.4 in `tuning.ts`) with their Gear icons.
- **The grid:** columns 9–15 are empty. Haugar's east column (`hau_watch` 8,2, `hau_huscarl` 8,3, `hau_tarn` 8,4) has a rock edge, so the way in is cut through one of them.
- **Breakables:** a `crack` (wall or rock) opens to a blast. Pits are solid ground-level holes nothing falls into, so "weak floors" are built as cracks over a hidden way down, not as falls.
- **Ground cover:** `drift` already says "blasts: true", so the hammer counts as a blast on drifts.
- **Water and current:** the `current` terrain field pushes swimmers only. Belts are a new field on terrain that pushes walkers.
- **Trades:** `q_trade` is at 5 (`trade_needle`, "for a dwarf who mends bellows"); stage 7 already reads "Every trade is made".
- **Seiðr vessels:** three of four are given (Gyða, Embla's first letter, the loom).
- **The farm:** `q_farm` stops at 2 with "The rest must wait for ore."
- **Captives left:** Ása, Bjarni, Þorkell and Rannveig. `q_captives` is at 4 after D5.
- **Save:** `SAVE_VERSION` stays 1. Every M8 id is appended. Heat, the escort's position and belts' pushes live on entities and the clock, never in `GameState`. `GameState`, `SaveData`, `Sim` and `ContentDb` keep their shape; the new thing kinds and terrain fields are append-only and listed in the plan first.

### Decisions (Claude's calls, taken)
- **Where Dvergagröf sits:** grid columns 9–11, rows 2–5. The way in is `hau_tarn`'s east edge, opened onto a chasm in `dvg_chasm` that only the grapple crosses (two posts over the drop). That keeps the gate the GDD names (the grapple chain) and puts Dvergagröf after D4. Its north edge stays shut by the killing frost until M9 (Hrímfjöll sits north of it).
- **Heat:** a screen or room marked `hot` fills a heat bar while Ask stands in it. Full heat burns half a heart every second until Ask cools off (out of the hot room, or in a water trough). The tunic and byrnie last 8 s; the ember byrnie 20 s. Outdoors only the vents at `dvg_vents` are hot. In D6 every forge room is hot, so the byrnie helps but is never required: the solver proves D6 without it.
- **The smith Sindri** at the camp forges for ore and silver: the ember byrnie (12 ore, 200 silver) and the dwarf-forged blade (16 ore, 300 silver, after D6's mini-boss, since it needs Belgr's iron heart). Both are optional for M8. The ember byrnie is the Hrímfjöll gate in M9.
- **Ore:** black rocks on three Dvergagröf screens (bombs), the escort's reward, D6 chests, and Hreggviðr's trades. Ore also pays for farm stage 3 (see captives).
- **The foreman Dvalinn's chain (`q_foreman`):**
  1. Clear the cave-in at the minehead (bombs, in any season).
  2. Escort his daughter Hekla through the old workings to the lamp-room, where the miners are trapped.
  3. The crew comes out, and Dvalinn opens the cart road (`dvg_int_tunnel`) from the minehead to Uppvík's smiths, both ways.
- **Escort AI:** Hekla follows Ask's trail a few tiles behind, stops when a foe is near her, and has her own small health. Foes go for her too. If she falls, she runs back to the camp and the escort restarts from the minehead; nothing is lost. She waits when Ask leaves her far behind.
- **The hammer:** a short, heavy blow on the item button. It drives stakes flat (they block the way until hammered, and come back up only on a switch), breaks weak floors (cracks with the art `floor`, over a way down), breaks drifts, and strikes armoured foes, whom the sword only glances off.
- **Skjálfti** (5 seiðr): the ground shakes. Every foe on the screen is staggered for 2 s, bosses shrug it off unless they are on their own anvil, and every weak floor on the screen breaks. Taught by a carved stave in D6 after the hammer; Hreggviðr trades `stave_skjalfti` for 2 ore, so it can be sampled first.
- **Lava:** a new terrain, solid like a pit. Ís lays a crust on it that can be walked for 6 s (lava never freezes for good); Eldr does nothing to it. A foe shoved into lava by Vindr burns.
- **Belts:** a new terrain field that pushes walkers (and props) one way at 1 px a tick. A lever (a switch) turns every belt on the screen the other way while its flag is set.
- **Captives:** D6 holds Þorkell and Rannveig, the neighbours. Killing Ívaldi frees them, and `q_captives` reaches 6. Þorkell comes home and takes ore for farm stage 3 (the byre and goats, 10 ore), so `q_farm` can go on; Rannveig sells arrows and bombs at her door. Ása and Bjarni wait for D7.
- **Thanes:** `st_thane_ivaldi`, and `q_thanes` reaches 3. Kolbeinn: "One left in the ice. Do you know what you are freeing, farmhand?"
- **Trading chain step 6:** the sail-needle goes to Sindri, who mends his bellows with it and gives a dwarf-glass lens (`trade_lens`): "for whoever watches the frost on the high road". Step 7 (the beacon ring) is M9's.
- **Embla's second letter** (`q_letters` 2): given at the Refuge once Dvergagröf is reached. It leads to the cairn on Haugar's tarn where she and Ask once hid from Halvar, and a heart piece is under its top stone (`hp_hau_tarn_letter`). The fourth seiðr vessel waits for M9 or M10.
- **Main quest `q_forge`** ("The forge under the mountain"):
  1. The chasm crossed.
  2. Dvergagröf reached, and Dvalinn met.
  3. Ívaldi's Forge entered.
  4. The hammer won.
  5. Ívaldi dead.
  6. Final stage text: "Three thanes down. The last one waits in the Rime Tower, and the frost on the high road will kill without the ember byrnie."

### Ívaldi's Forge (D6, 28 rooms, M8b)
- **Theme:** the corrupted dwarf king's forge inside the mountain. Lava channels, belts that carry ore carts and Ask, stakes, weak floors and hot rooms.
- **The new mechanic, the hammer:** taught in a safe room right after Belgr, tested in three rooms (stakes, a weak floor, an armoured foe), then combined with the grapple and belts before the boss. Skjálfti comes later from a stave behind a lava crossing (Ís crust), and breaks a whole floor of weak tiles.
- **The loop:**
  1. The map and compass.
  2. The belts' lever, which turns the north belts.
  3. Belgr guards the hammer.
  4. Stakes and weak floors open the way to the big key; Skjálfti's stave on the way.
  5. Ívaldi.
- **Extras:** one heart piece behind an optional belt puzzle (`hp_d6_r21`), a secret room with an ore cache (weak floor), the heart container `d6_hc`, and captive cells with Þorkell and Rannveig.
- **Ívaldi, the Anvil** (a corrupted dwarf king in plated armour, boss bar "Ívaldi", 3 phases):
  1. He walks the floor and brings his hammer down in a shockwave line (tell: hammer raised). Four armour plates cover him; the hammer breaks one each time he stands stunned after a missed blow.
  2. At 2/3 health he climbs his anvil in the middle and the belts start. Skjálfti shakes him off the anvil, and he lies open for the hammer.
  3. At 1/3 health he glows white-hot and the floor's lava rises at the edges. Ís cools him for 3 s, and only then does the hammer bite.
  - On his death: the heart container, a warp point at the forge gate, the captives' chains break, and Kolbeinn's line.
