M10 is Act III:
- With all four thanes dead, Halvar comes up to Útgarðr's gate and confesses what his generation did.
- His old binding-words open the gate. Ask fights through the giants' stronghold, one wing for each act's tools.
- Ask beats Kolbeinn, and spares or kills him.
- With Embla holding the binding open, Ask fights Hrímnir the Rime King.
- The ending follows: the villagers home, spring on the mountains, and Embla's question (stay, or go).

### What the code check turned up (it shapes the design)
- **Ids already there:** dungeon `d8`, flag `st_utgard_open` (not set yet), screen `hrf_utgard` (13,0) with a rime gate on `st_utgard_open`, NPCs `kolbeinn` and `embla`, and credits support (a `credits` script step and `content/credits.ts`, written for the demo).
- **No "master key" item exists.** Dungeons already carry a `big_key`. D8's big key *is* the master key: its chest text names it, and its lock is on the keep.
- **Parry exists** (Styrr's duel, `huscarl.ts`, `parryStun`).
- **A duel's "death is a yield"** (`EnemyDef.duel`) resets the fight if Ask loses. Kolbeinn is a real boss instead, and his death opens a choice.
- **Hrímfjöll's season is fixed to winter** (`ClockRules.fixedSeason`). Spring on the mountains needs that rule to follow a flag.
- **Halvar's confession** from the GDD's Act II ("he fought the Rime King before, and his generation chose to bind rather than kill") has not been written yet. Only a hint exists, in the mail-clasp line.
- **Save:** `SAVE_VERSION` stays 1, and every M10 id is appended. The binding clock and the arena live on the runtime and on entities. `GameState`, `SaveData`, `Sim` and `ContentDb` keep their shape.

### Decisions (Claude's calls, taken)

**Halvar's confession opens the gate.**
- After the fourth thane falls, Halvar walks up to `hrf_utgard` and waits by the gate.
- Talking to him plays his confession: he was a huscarl at the binding, and they chose to bind rather than kill.
- Then he speaks the binding-words, and the rime gate melts (`st_halvar_confessed`, `st_utgard_open`).
- The Refuge and Askdalr get new lines about where he has gone.

**Útgarðr (D8), 40 rooms.** It sits on its own 8×5 dungeon grid behind the gate. It has three wings, each one "an act":

| Wing | Tools | Ends at |
| --- | --- | --- |
| West wing (Act I) | Boomerang, bombs, bow, fire | A seal-stone |
| East wing (Act II) | Grapple, Vindr, the hammer, Ís | A seal-stone |
| High wing (Act III) | Glaze, beams and the mirror, Bragð | A seal-stone |

- **Seal-stones:** each is an eye-like stone set by a beam or a blow (`switch`-style latch flags `st_d8_seal_w/e/n`). All three open the vault of the master key.
- **Jötunvörðr**, a frost-giant warden, guards the master key. It stomps (shockwave ring, tell: knee raised), and is open only while thawed with Eldr. It is the mini-boss.
- **The master key** opens the keep: Kolbeinn's hall, then the binding hall where Hrímnir lies.
- **Keys:** four small keys, one heart piece behind an optional puzzle (`hp_d8_keep`), and one secret room with an arm-ring cache (silver, since every ring is taken).

**The room-template library.** `content/world/utgard/templates.ts` holds typed functions that build a room's map from parts: a base with exits, pillars, a glaze rink, an eye niche, a cell, a lava channel, a pit band. The 40 rooms are written with it in TypeScript, not generated by a script outside the repo. Later content can reuse it.

**Kolbeinn's duel.** A parry-focused duel:
1. He strikes with his seiðr-staff in a three-blow chain (tell: the staff drawn back). A parry staggers him, and the sword bites.
2. At half health he adds a rime bolt that only the mirror or a parry turns.
3. At a quarter he summons two draugr. The duel goes on while they live.
- At 0 he falls to one knee, and a choice follows:
  - **Spare him:** he leaves, and his last line comes in the epilogue.
  - **Kill him:** `st_kolbeinn_slain`.
- Either way the keep's inner door opens. The choice changes only one ending scene, as the GDD says.

**Hrímnir, the Rime King.** Three phases in the binding hall. Embla stands at the hall's south end, holding the binding open with the runes of her blood (she comes in when Ask enters, scripted). The binding is a glowing ring on the floor: inside it, Ask is safe; outside it, the killing frost bites. The ring shrinks over each phase. That is the clock. If it closes all the way, Hrímnir's breath knocks Ask back to the ring's edge (heavy damage), and the ring opens wide again. That is a punishment, not an instant loss.
1. **Phase 1:** he sweeps a hand across the hall (tell: the arm drawn back, shadowed). A struck hand stuns him for a moment, and his heart-rune glows on his chest, open to arrows and Bragð.
2. **Phase 2** (2/3 health): he breathes rime in a cone. The mirror turns the breath back onto his heart-rune.
3. **Phase 3** (1/3 health): the new pattern. He pulls the hall's rime pillars down (shadows), and each fallen pillar is glaze that Ask must slide over to reach him.
- On his death: the binding closes for good, `st_hrimnir_dead`, and the ending plays.

**The ending.**
- **Spring on the mountains:** `ClockRules.fixedSeason` gains a flag, and Hrímfjöll follows the world's season once `st_hrimnir_dead` is set.
- **Epilogue scenes**, as scripts at the Refuge and in Askdalr:
  1. The villagers come home.
  2. The farm, rebuilt to the stage `q_farm` reached (the goat-house if it is 3).
  3. Kolbeinn's scene: spared, he is seen walking south alone; slain, Gyða cuts his name into the record.
  4. Embla on the shore with her boat: "Come with me?"
- **The choice:** stay (`st_end_stay`) or go (`st_end_go`) picks the closing shot. Then the final credits.
- **After the credits:** Ask wakes at the farm on the next spring morning, and the world stays open (a post-game for M11's completion work). The save after the ending keeps `st_game_done`. The title screen shows a small rune once the game is done.

2. **Does the game end at the credits?** No: Ask wakes on the farm, and the world stays open for completion (**taken**).
